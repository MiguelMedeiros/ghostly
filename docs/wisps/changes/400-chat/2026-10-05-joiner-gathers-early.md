The joiner of a first pairing gathers its WebRTC offer while it reads the inviter's record for the first time, and sends it once that read finds the inviter. Before, it gathered only after the read, so the read (0.15 to 0.8 s) and the gathering (0.2 to 2 s) came one after the other. A read that finds nobody drops the gathered offer without saying anything. In fake time, with reads of 0.75 s and STUN answering in 1.5 s: live 4.4 s after the join at the desktop's pace and 4.85 s at the relays' pace, instead of 5.4 and 6.75 s, with fewer reads.
