A reaction to a message that is not here yet now waits for it five minutes, at most 64 per sender in each chat, where it waited a minute, at most 64 per chat. A member back in a busy private group hears every member's latest reactions as their edges open, before the catch-up brings the messages they name: seven members filled the one room of 64 at once, and a catch-up from one author takes over a minute, so most of those reactions were dropped and came again only at the next edge opening. A 1:1 chat has one sender, so only the time changes there. Nothing on the wire changes; an app from before keeps its minute and its room per chat.
