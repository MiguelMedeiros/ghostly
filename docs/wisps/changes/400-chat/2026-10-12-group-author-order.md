Requirement 10 gets one exception: in a private group, a member's messages read in the order that member wrote them (the epoch and number it signs on each, never a time). When one comes of a member whose later messages were placed in the last three minutes, those are placed again below it, in the order written. Two catch-up answers can race, the author's own newest 32 and another member's hand-on of the older ones, and each is in order alone: a member back after 200 messages of one author read that author's 168th last and the 200th thirty rows up, or, with every edge opening at once, the newest 32 first and the older ones after them in blocks of 32, newest first. A row only moves down, past what came after it, never back among older messages, so nobody else's lines change order and what the reader sent stays where it was. Something older than what was placed more than three minutes ago is placed where it comes, as before. Nothing on the wire changes.
