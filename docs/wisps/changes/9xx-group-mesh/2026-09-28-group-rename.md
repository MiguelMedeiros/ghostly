The admin renames the group: a `name` in the metadata beside the picture and the hubs, signed and sealed like them, one clean line of 1 to 64 characters and 256 bytes that a receiver ignores rather than cuts when it does not hold. A member without it shows its first name, the one the group was created with or its invitation and welcome said, which is also what apps from before names show; a rename keeps the picture and a new picture keeps the name (#438).
