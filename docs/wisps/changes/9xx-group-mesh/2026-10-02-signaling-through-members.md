Edge signaling through members: `group-signal` carries an edge's own signed Pkarr packet over a link that is already open (the entry session that admitted the member, the contact chat of an invitation, or a member that reaches both ends), passed on once and taken like a relay's answer; the relays get only the newest packet, 1.5 s later, unless the edge opened meanwhile. The joiner keeps its entry session until its edge with the admin is up. `paired-groups` version 5.
