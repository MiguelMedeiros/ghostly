A member's own frames that no edge took when it said them are said again once it has caught up and its chain has settled for five seconds, when a member now in was let in no later than the frame: the same text, boxes and time, sealed under the newest epoch before any admission dated after the frame, with `o` (where the first was said, covered by `xs`). Receivers take it as the first message, by the first one's id, and one that read the first does not show it twice; an edit of the message also goes as an edit of the copy to its readers.
