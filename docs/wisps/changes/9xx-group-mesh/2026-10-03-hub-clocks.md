Hubs: a reader learns each hub's clock from its beacon entry as a community's reader does (the entry moved between two readings, here at most 75 seconds apart since a member reads once a minute) and, when the two clocks are more than 45 seconds apart, reads that hub's time and `since` moved by the difference; what is written back is each entry as its hub dated it, and a member dates its lobby request by the hub's clock. Before, a member whose clock was 90 seconds or more from the hubs' saw no hub in the beacon, and a hub with such a clock was a hub to nobody.
