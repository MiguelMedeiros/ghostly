A member removed while its app was closed, with no contact chat to carry the notice (it came through the group's link), was never told: every member closes its edge to someone out of the roster, so the group stayed active on its device, and what it wrote went nowhere. The admin that removed it now keeps its edge to that member until the edge opens, sends the `remove` commit again over it (and, to a member further behind, the commits between, without secrets), and closes it 15 seconds later, or after a week (§ Admission and departure). Nothing else goes over that edge, and nothing else is taken from it. The edge closes a minute at most after its connection opened, told or not. What a group says goes over the edges of its current members only (§ Admission and departure, Current members only): an edge still there to someone out of the roster carries the commits up to the one that took them out.
