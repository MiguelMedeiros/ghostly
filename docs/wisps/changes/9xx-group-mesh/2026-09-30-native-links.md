A group's links (edges and entry sessions) go over Iroh or HyperDHT with a member whose app has no WebRTC, such as Ghostly Desktop on Linux (§ Transports). A group link has no capability record, so an app with no WebRTC says in the link's own packet, under the new label `_tr`, which transports it runs and how to dial its native ones, and a member's app that has WebRTC answers in kind. Two apps that both have WebRTC never publish `_tr`, and apps from before ignore it.
