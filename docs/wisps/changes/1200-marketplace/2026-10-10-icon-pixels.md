An app's `icon.png` is now also bounded in pixels: at most 1024 on a side. Until now only its bytes were bounded (256 KiB) and its header had to say it is square, so a small file could name a picture of tens of thousands of pixels on a side, which a client decodes whole to draw the icon on the install screen. A reader refuses a larger icon as `bad-icon` in step 9 of the check order, with the square check, and a writer refuses to build it. The package table, the paths rule and the `app-bundle.json` vectors say so (the bundle at its limits has an icon of 1024 × 1024; one of 1025 × 1025 and one of 23000 × 23000 are refused). A bundle whose icon is larger must be published again with a smaller one.
