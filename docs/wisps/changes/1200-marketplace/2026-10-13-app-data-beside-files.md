An app's data keeps its own quarter of the session's channel while a file goes: while files wrote on the session or waited for room on it in the last 2 seconds, `chat.send` is refused with `too-fast` only past what files may hold plus a quarter (on a budget channel, half the budget; on WebRTC, `LIMITS.sendHighWaterMark` and a quarter). Files keep the channel at their own line for the whole transfer (1 MiB on WebRTC, a quarter of the budget elsewhere), which was also the app's line, so every app frame was refused until the file ended: a Chess move waited 10 seconds and gave up on a 512 KiB/s WebRTC link with a 16 MiB file, and 118 of 120 checks were refused on a real Chromium data channel. With its own quarter, every move went at once. Without a file the line is a quarter, as before. No frame and no wire change.
