The sender now keeps an app's data to a share of the session's channel as well as to 48 frames a second: while the channel already holds a quarter of what it may queue (a quarter of its send budget, or of `LIMITS.sendHighWaterMark` on a WebRTC channel, which has none), `chat.send` is refused with `too-fast`, as files keep to a quarter of the budget (501, Backpressure). Within the 48 frames a second alone, an app sending 32 KiB frames over a 512 KiB/s WebRTC link filled Chromium's 16 MiB send queue, and the chat's messages waited up to 32 seconds behind its data; with the room check they waited under half a second. No frame and no code change: `too-fast` already tells the app to back off and send again.
