The runner now says `activity` to the broker for the person's taps and keys in the app (a trusted `pointerdown` or `keydown`, at most one every 5 seconds), because input in the app's frame or window never reaches the client's page and its lock screen locked a person out of a game longer than the lock time. The broker answers it and counts it for the lock only while the person is in the app: its frame has the focus in a page that has it (web), or its window has the focus and is not minimized (Desktop). The app runs in the runner's realm and can send `activity` itself, so an app the person has focused can keep the lock away while they stay in it; leaving it lets the lock come as before. The runner-only row of the message table names it.
