Ghostly's default store is the repository github.com/MiguelMedeiros/ghostly-store, read at `HEAD` from `raw.githubusercontent.com` (an index pins its bytes by signature, `sequence` and `expires`, and a commit-pinned copy would never move). The client pins its public key, and keeps the default store off while that key is not set. Each app has a folder `apps/<name>.<prefix>/`, `<prefix>` being the first 16 characters of the publisher key; first-party apps such as Chess are hosted there beside their listing, while other publishers host their bundle in their own repository. The owner signs indexes 80 days ahead at most, so a client whose clock is up to 10 days late still reads them under the 90-day rule. The store's CI checks each listing's bundles with `ghostly app verify` and refuses an index that a client would refuse, a lower or equal `sequence` with other bytes, and a new store key.
