`ghostly.files.list()` answers are now counted in the app's file budget with `file` and `files.read` (16 MiB a second, `MINI_APP_LIMITS.fileBytesPerSecond`), by at least the bytes the answer takes as JSON: each entry's `path` and 80 for the rest of it. A list that starts under the budget is answered whole; past it `files.list` is refused with `too-fast` until the second has gone. Within the 50 requests a second alone, an app keeping 100,000 links (about 15 MB a list with library-like paths) could make the page copy 758 MB a second.
