An app's data no longer takes the whole link from a file: while files wait for room on the session, `chat.send` is also refused with `too-fast` once the app has sent 16 KiB more than the file since it last got ahead (`APP_AHEAD_OF_FILES`), and a file's bytes take that back as they go. Before, the app's quarter above files' line was refilled each time the channel drained to that line, so an app at its allowed 50 frames of 32 KiB a second kept a file from moving: 21 KiB of a file went in 10 s on a 512 KiB/s Iroh channel and 1 MiB on WebRTC, where about 5 MiB went alone. Now the file and the app each get about half (2.5 MiB each in the same 10 s), and a small frame such as a Chess move still goes at once. No frame and no wire change.
