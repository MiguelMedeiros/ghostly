Proposal, not accepted and with nothing built: a new section designs phase 2, real-time apps (Pong, Tron, a Quake-like), their data and apps in groups. It lays out messages that may be lost (a `lossy` call of at most 1024 bytes, on a second unordered WebRTC channel, Iroh's QUIC datagrams or HyperDHT's unordered messages, and what to do on a relay that has none), a session capability `apps-rt/1`, the `realtime` permission with its install line and receiver limits, bundles up to 64 MiB and where a store finds one past jsDelivr's 20 MB, a library of files the person picks for an app (a game's own data, read-only for the app, per app), and tables in groups (a star to the member who opened the app, a mesh of players or the hubs). It records what was measured in the frame on Chromium 153 and WebKitGTK 2.52.6: WebGL, WebAssembly, sound after a click, keys and the gamepad call work as the frame is; pointer lock needs `allow-pointer-lock` in both the frame's attribute and the runner's header; full screen is refused in the frame and can be the client's own. It ends with an order of work and ten questions for the owner. The games table and the phases table point to it; no rule of phase 1 changes.
