In phase 1 a pasted URL in the Apps page is read only from the hosts a card may name (`raw.githubusercontent.com`, and `cdn.jsdelivr.net` pinned to a commit), not from any HTTPS host: another host comes once the Apps page can name who will learn the person's address before the request. A client reads at most 4 MiB of a store index, counted on the body as it arrives after any compression is undone (a page cannot see the compressed size), and refuses a larger one; the format's bound stays 16 MiB.
