The manifest gains an optional `view`: `chat`, the default, for an app that runs only inside a 1:1 chat, such as Chess, and `full` for a full-screen app. Any other value is refused with `bad-field`. The Apps page asks which chat to open a `chat` app in instead of opening it alone, and opens a `full` app alone. Opened from a chat, a `full` app covers the whole chat at every width, keeps the contact, the connection and Back to the chat, and never sits beside it. On Desktop a `full` app's window opens at the Ghostly window's size. The Manifest table and the Per client rows say so, and the bundle vectors gain a `view` and an unknown view.
