A shared service's body going on the session now counts as files do for an app's data: its chunks written and its waits for room keep the app's quarter above the line the body keeps to, and the app's 16 KiB ahead while the body waits. Before, on a WebRTC channel, which has no send budget, a body refilled the channel to `LIMITS.sendHighWaterMark` each time it drained to its low water mark, and the app's quarter sat below that: on a 512 KiB/s link a Chess move waited 2.6 to 10 s while the contact loaded 8 MiB from the service, and an app at its limit got 10 KiB/s. Now a move goes at once and the app and the body each get about half. No frame and no wire change.
