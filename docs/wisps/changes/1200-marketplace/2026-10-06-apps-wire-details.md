The `apps/1` wire is pinned for its first implementation. A `paired-app` frame has exactly one of `o` and `d`, and `v` is required on `open` as a semantic version of at most 64 characters. A receiver also drops an `open` for a 17th app while the peer has 16 open on a session, and counts every frame against the 50 a second per app before reading it. A sender sends at most 48 data frames a second per app, so an `open` or a `close` always fits. When a session ends, each app the peer had open hears that it closed, marked `offline`. `chat.send` names why a frame did not go: `not-open`, `too-large`, `offline`, `peer-closed` or `too-fast`.
