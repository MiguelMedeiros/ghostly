The readers now refuse `__proto__`, `constructor` and `prototype` (`unknown-key`) anywhere in a manifest, a store index, a listing and a removal, as the format already said ("at any level"), including inside the value of a key they ignore, where they used to refuse them only at the levels they read: one walk over the parsed JSON, before any field is read, finds them at any depth. The refusal's detail names the key's path (`later.0.__proto__`). The vectors add `__proto__` and `constructor` two levels inside a later key of a manifest, an index, a listing and a `listing.json`.
