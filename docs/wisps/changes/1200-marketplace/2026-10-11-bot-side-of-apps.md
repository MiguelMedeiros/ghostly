The bot side of `apps/1` comes forward from phase 2, so that an agent on the CLI can play Chess with a contact, and "A bot on the other side" now says how it works. A bot serves an app from its signed bundle (`app serve <bundle|url>`, checked as a client checks one), not from a bare reference: the app card needs the bundle's digest, `sequence` and title. Each permission the manifest asks is answered by a `--grant`, and `internet` cannot be granted. Either side may open first: `app open` says `open` and sends the app card unless the contact's app is open already, and a served app never opens by itself. The events are `app.opened`, `app.closed` and `app.message`, and `app send` fails with the sender's reasons of "In a chat". The CLI row of "Per client" says a profile that serves an app offers `apps/1` and answers the chat calls only, with no store. Nothing changes on the wire. The `cards` permission stays phase 2.
