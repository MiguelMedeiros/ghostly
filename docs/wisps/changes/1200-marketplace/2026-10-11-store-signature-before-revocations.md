A reader checks a store index's own signature before the signatures of the revocations it carries. Reading, in order, is now: size, JSON, canonical bytes, the fields with the shape of each `revoked` entry, the signature statement, the store key the reader holds, the index's signature, the signature of each `revoked` entry, then `expires`. Before, every revocation's signature was checked with the fields, ahead of the index's own: an index of 4096 revocations cost its reader 4096 signature checks before the reader knew whether the store had signed it. Every index accepted before is accepted now and every one refused is still refused; only an index with two faults can get another code: a revocation that does not verify in an index whose own signature does not verify is `bad-signature` (it was `bad-revocation`), and under another key than the reader holds it is `store-key`. A reader that shares its thread with a page checks an index a few milliseconds at a time, and need not check again a revocation it has verified. `app-store.json` pins the new order with four cases.
