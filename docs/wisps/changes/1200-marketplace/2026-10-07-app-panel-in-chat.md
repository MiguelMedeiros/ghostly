On the web, an app opened in a 1:1 chat runs in a panel of that chat instead of over the whole page. On a wide screen the panel sits beside the chat, which stays usable, and it can take the chat's whole width. On a phone it covers the chat, and Back returns to the chat while the app keeps running. The panel's header has the app's name, the contact and the chat's own connection control, so the person always sees who they play with and whether the chat is live. An app opened alone is still full screen. The Per client table says so.
