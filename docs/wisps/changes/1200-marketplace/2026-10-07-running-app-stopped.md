An app that is already running when an update check finds its version removed by a store or revoked by its publisher is stopped too, not only the next time it opens. The client refuses its storage, its files and its frames to the contact (`open` and data) with `stopped` from then on, whatever its frame does, and tells the pages of every update check, the scheduled one included, so they read the apps again and close the frame. A run the person started with "Run anyway" goes on after a removal; nothing goes on after a revocation. Exporting the app's data stays possible.
