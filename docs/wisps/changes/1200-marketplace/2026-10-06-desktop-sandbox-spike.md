The Desktop sandbox spike measured the runner on WKWebView (macOS 15.6) with a malicious app in an `app-*` window on the `ghostly-app` scheme, against local listeners and a control with no policy. The CSP `sandbox` header from a Tauri custom scheme is honoured (origin `"null"`, no storage, no service worker); fetch, XHR, images, CSS, fonts, scripts, frames, media, forms, WebSocket, EventSource, `sendBeacon`, workers, `import()` and `<a ping>` reach nothing; `app_broker` reaches Rust through Tauri's `postMessage` fallback and knows the app by its window label, and every other command and plugin is refused there; top navigation, reloads, `window.open` and `target=_blank` are stopped by `on_navigation` and the sandbox. Two channels were open under the policy and are now closed: `<link rel=preconnect>` opened a connection, and a `srcdoc` frame had its own `RTCPeerConnection` and reached STUN and TURN, so the text no longer says the opaque origin protects nested frames. On Desktop `on_navigation` now refuses `about:srcdoc` and `about:blank` frames, and on macOS the window gets a content rule list that blocks every load but `ghostly-app:` and WebKit's preconnect, DNS prefetch, prefetch and WebRTC features off; a per-window proxy was measured and does nothing in WKWebView. WebKitGTK, WebView2 and nested frames on the web are still to be measured.
