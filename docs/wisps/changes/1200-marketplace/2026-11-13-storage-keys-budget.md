`ghostly.storage.keys()` answers are now counted in the app's 16 MiB a second of answers with `file`, `files.read`, `files.list` and `remote.read` (`MINI_APP_LIMITS.fileBytesPerSecond`), by the bytes the list takes as JSON, escapes included. A list that starts under the budget is answered whole; past it `storage.keys` is refused with `too-fast` until the second has gone. A key is still bounded by its 256 UTF-8 bytes, but a control character takes six as JSON, so a full scope's list can reach about 3.7 MB, and within the 50 requests a second alone an app could make the client write about 185 MB of JSON a second.
