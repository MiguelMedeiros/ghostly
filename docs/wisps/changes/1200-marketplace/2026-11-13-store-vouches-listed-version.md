A curated store clears "Unknown publisher" and names itself ("In <store>") only for the version it lists, its listing's digest. Another version of the same app, from a contact's card or a pasted link, shows both warnings as an app the store does not list, on the install screen and on its row once installed. An app installed from a store keeps that store's name while the store lists the app, also when its listing moved on to a newer version the app has not taken yet.
