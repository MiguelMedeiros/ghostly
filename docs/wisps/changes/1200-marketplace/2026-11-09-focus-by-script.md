The focus of the page and an app's frame on the web, in The runner and the broker. An app's frame gets the focus only from the person (a click or a tap on the app, or a Tab into it): a frame that takes it by script is refused and the focus goes back where it was. New: an app refused 3 times in a row, with no click, tap or Tab into it in between, is stopped, and its place says it kept taking the keyboard. An app that focuses a field of its own when it opens is refused once and keeps running. No frame or message changes.
