Three rules from the review of the first implementation: the owner refuses a request dated more than ten minutes ahead of its clock (it would wait past its seven days), and one whose invite is a chat it made or already joined (accepting would rename that chat and put the request's text in it); a request that ended unanswered leaves no trace of its asker, so asking again is read as any other; accepted and declined keys are kept, the latest 4,096. No wire change.
