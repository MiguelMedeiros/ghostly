First draft: a contact link, `ghostly1z…` (version 2 of the `ghostly1` code, 32 bytes: a link key that names nobody), that anyone may open many times. Opening it sends the owner a request (a name, a short text and an invite of its own) sealed to the link key under the requester's own Pkarr key, rung in through four shared knock slots with a small proof of work; the owner accepts by joining that invite, or declines and nothing is said back. The limits on requests waiting, taken per hour and read per key, the `busy` and `off` states, and what each side learns before an accept are set here. Planned for 1.4; nothing is built yet.
