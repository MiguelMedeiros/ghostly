Occupancy: a member signs `POST <sfu>/sfu/group-occupancy` (tag `"ghostly-sfu/1 occupancy"`, the token request's fields without `name`) to learn whether the group's call is on before joining it. The node checks the token request's five checks, rate-limits apart from the token route, and answers `{ "count", "since" }`: how many roster keys are in `"g_" + g` and since when the room is open, never who. The app asks while the group is open and in sight, at most once every 15 seconds, and treats a `404`, a refusal or no answer as not knowing. With a test vector, Compatibility, Security and Conformance notes; the route is optional on both sides.
