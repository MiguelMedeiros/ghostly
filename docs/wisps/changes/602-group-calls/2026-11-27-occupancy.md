Occupancy: a member signs `POST <sfu>/sfu/group-occupancy` (tag `"ghostly-sfu/1 occupancy"`, the token request's fields without `name`) to learn whether the group's call is on before joining it. The node checks the token request's five checks, refuses a key banned from the call with the same `403`, rate-limits apart from the token route (30 a minute per `k`, 240 per address), exposes `Retry-After` and caches its preflight, and answers `{ "count", "since" }`: how many roster keys other than its own are in `"g_" + g` and since the earliest of their joins, never who. The app asks while the group is open and in sight, at most once every 15 seconds, treats a `404`, a `405`, a refusal or no answer as not knowing, backs off up to 10 minutes on no answer (an old node refuses the browser's preflight), and stops on a `404` or a `405`. With a test vector, Compatibility, Security and Conformance notes; the route is optional on both sides and not built yet.
