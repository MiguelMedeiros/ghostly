Draft: a community group names the node that forwards its calls with `call: { sfu: "<https origin>" }` in its admin-signed metadata, and a member asks that node for a LiveKit pass with `POST <sfu>/sfu/group-token`, signed by its group key over `["ghostly-sfu/1 token", sfuOrigin, g, room, k, ts, n]` (with a fixed test vector). The room is `"g_" + g`; the node checks the time within 60 s, a nonce not seen in 120 s, the signature and the roster, answers every refusal with one `403` and `429` past 30 a minute, and allows any origin (CORS). End-to-end encryption of the media is optional, turned on by `e2ee: 1` in `call`, with a key derived from the group's epoch secret. Not built yet.
