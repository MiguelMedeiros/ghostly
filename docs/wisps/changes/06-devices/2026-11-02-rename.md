Rename is built, as User experience listed it: on the active device every row's menu has Rename, this device's own included, and the name goes into the next record of the turn (`rev` plus one), with nothing else changed. No format changes: every app reads names from the record's slots, and a standby takes the new name at its next read, and its screen follows it. The active device sends its turn hint over every live link after a rename, and a hint that comes inside the 30 seconds after the last read is now read at their end instead of dropped. A name the slot would change is refused rather than cleaned.
