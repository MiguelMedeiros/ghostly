Try again on the active device after a move that stopped now reaches the other device. The active device often sees the copy stop first, and its cancel can be lost with the link, so the standby was still in the earlier attempt when the new offer came, and dropped it. Now a standby in an attempt that has no release yet takes an offer from that device with another handoff's id in its place (A offers only when it runs no handoff), keeping its staged files. The active device sends an offer not answered yet again on each new session of the link, and when the standby's hello or `handoff-have` shows an earlier attempt. A standby that released and never got `handoff-done` takes an offer from the device it released to as that done, and frames from that device go to its taker.
