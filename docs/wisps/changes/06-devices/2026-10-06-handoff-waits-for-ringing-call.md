A call ringing in on the active device now holds a handoff as a call that is on does: the request gets `handoff-busy` `call`, and Move to says why. Before, only a call placed or answered held it, so a move made while a call rang lost the call without a word: the ringing stopped on the old device, the caller rang on to its timeout and the new device only showed a missed call. The ring still does not count as a call on for one call at a time (WISP 601): it holds the device from the ring until the ring ends, declined, answered, rung out, given up by the caller or with its chat gone.
