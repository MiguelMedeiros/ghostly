A standby also reads the record on **Use here** when its link to the device it believes active is down. A device removed while its standby screen stayed open read the record only every 10 minutes, so its Use here tried for 40 seconds and said the active device could not be reached, while that device was on and had removed it. The read now finds the tombstone first, and the screen says the device was removed. A pull over a live link reads nothing more.
