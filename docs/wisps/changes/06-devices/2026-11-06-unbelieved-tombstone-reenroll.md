A device with no copy that reads a valid tombstone that does not list it, signed by a device other than the one it believes active, is still `moving` and not `removed`, and now also offers to be added again. That tombstone may be the real one: the profile moved to another device while this one was off (a handoff or a takeover it never read), and that device then removed it. The remover sends no frame to a device it removed, and the device this one believes signs nothing any more, so the wait had no end. A `set-update` signed by the device it believes is still accepted and takes the offer away; a forged tombstone still ends nothing.
