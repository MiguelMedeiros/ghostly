A device link session replaced under a handoff now reaches it. When the other device dialled in again, the data link never closed and the capabilities agreed did not change, so neither side heard of the new session: a push's request sent on the replaced session was lost, the active device kept waiting on its offer and the taker waited for a copy that never came, then stalled. The device links now tell the handoff that the old session ended and the new one opened when `handoff/1` is agreed on another session. An active device that hears hello for its offer on a later session than the first sends the offer again, and a taker of a push with no manifest yet that sees its own offer again sends the request again on that session, once per session. Also, a push's taker no longer arms the password proof's one-minute wait, which ended every push that took longer as "dropped".
