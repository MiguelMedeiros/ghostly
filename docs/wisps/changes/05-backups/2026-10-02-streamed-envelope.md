Envelope version 2: a bundle is a header line followed by frames, each sealed on its own (AES-256-GCM per frame, the key derived from the passphrase as before) and ended by a final marker, so it is written and read a piece at a time and a cut or changed bundle is refused. Files of any size now travel in a bundle with their bytes; before, files over 16 MiB were left out. A bundle can be made without a passphrase by explicit choice (`protection: none`, checked by a SHA-256 chain, never sent to remote storage). A restore that fails or is cancelled takes back what it wrote, and clients show progress with Cancel. Version 1 bundles still restore. The text now lists what a bundle does not hold.
