Client policy, no wire change: as layer 1 comes up the reader looks at the contact's mailbox once more, so a text the contact put on the DHT in the seconds before (after the reader's last look) shows at once instead of at the next look 5 minutes later. The sender also sends again on the live session, under the same id, a text whose DHT publication was still under way when layer 1 came up; the reader keeps one copy by its id.
