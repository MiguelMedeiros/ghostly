Client policy, no wire change: a live session the contact closed (not one lost to liveness) gives one more read of its mailbox 4 s after the read at once, at most one a minute per chat. A contact that chooses DHT only closes the session first and puts its envelope saying so just after, so the read at once found the old envelope, and a chat in the background or on a daemon saw the choice, and the first text sent after it, only at its next 30 s background read (32 s each time on two CLI daemons; about 4 s now).
