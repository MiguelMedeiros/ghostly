A member asks a holder for one file at a time, and a holder's "one per asking member" no longer counts a transfer whose bytes all went. Files announced together were all asked of their author at once; the author served one and answered `busy` to the rest, which then waited a want's 30 seconds each. And the next want, sent on the group's frames as soon as the last file checked out, could reach the holder before that file's `pf-done` on files/3 and be answered `busy` too. Now the next file waits its turn on the asker's side and is asked when the one before ends, and the holder serves it. Nothing changes on the wire.
