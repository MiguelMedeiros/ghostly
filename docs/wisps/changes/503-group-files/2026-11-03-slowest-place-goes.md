Serving limits: when all three places are taken and a member holding none asks, or was answered `busy` in the last 30 seconds and waits, the place of the slowest transfer goes to it, as the one held 5 minutes does: a transfer under way for 10 seconds since its offer, measured by what it confirmed for the first time over the last 10 to 20 seconds, whose rest at that pace takes longer than it has been served and longer than 10 seconds. A member's transfer of a file loses its place this way once per holder; its asker goes on from what it stored, from another holder or this one again. Before, members that asked first and downloaded slowly kept all three places for their whole download, and members on fast links waited for them. Only the holder's side changes: an asker on any version sees a `pf-abort`, as for the 5-minute rule.
