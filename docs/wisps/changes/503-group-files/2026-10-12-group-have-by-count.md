A private group's `group-have` is paced by count, not by a fixed gap (§ Who holds what, § Bounds): what a member has to say on an edge is gathered for a second and then goes, at most 10 frames in 10 seconds on that edge, each of 32 ids as before. Before, an edge carried one frame every 10 seconds, so of several files sent together only the first was said at once: a member turned away as `busy`, or waiting for a new holder, heard whom to ask up to 10 seconds late, and the files reached a group of 16 in 10-second steps. Nothing changes for the reader of a `group-have`, and a community's stays at one every 60 seconds per member.
