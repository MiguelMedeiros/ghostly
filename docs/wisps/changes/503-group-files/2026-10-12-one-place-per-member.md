Serving limits: a member holds at most one of a holder's serving places, whatever it confirms. A transfer whose every byte the member confirmed is not its place only for 5 seconds after its last byte was confirmed (its `pf-done` may be overtaken by the next want), and once the next one is under way it ends 5 seconds after its last byte was confirmed if its `pf-done` has not come. A served transfer is let go after 30 seconds without a byte confirmed for the first time: accepting from an earlier offset is no progress. Before, a member that confirmed every byte and never said `pf-done` could keep several places with no end.
