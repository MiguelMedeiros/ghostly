Who may fetch a file whose announcement was said again: a holder decides by the epochs it has the announcement under, not by the message's time. The author serves the members of the epochs it sealed its copies under; any other holder, those of the epoch the announcement's frame reached it under, or of a copy that reached it after the first; always only members still in the roster. Before (revision 2026-10-19), a holder compared the message's time, from the author's clock, with the dates of the `add` commits, from the admin's, for every message. No frame changes; a holder that never got a copy said again serves only the message's own epoch, and the asker gets the file from the author or a holder that did.
