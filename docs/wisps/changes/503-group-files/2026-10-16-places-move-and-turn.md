Serving limits: a served transfer is let go after 30 seconds without 16 KiB (or the rest of its file) confirmed for the first time, not only without a byte. When all three places are taken and a member holding none asks, the transfer that has held its place longest goes to that member once it has held it for 5 minutes; its asker goes on from what it stored. Before, a few members that each confirmed a byte now and then could keep a holder's three places with no end.
