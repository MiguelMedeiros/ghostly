The reader of a private group's `group-have` takes at most as many frames from an edge as its sender may send (10 in 10 seconds) and drops the rest: each one makes its member worth asking again, so they are not taken faster than that. A holder whose bytes failed the author's digest is not asked for that file again until the person asks for it again, also when every byte stored came from it over two transfers, the first one stalled; only bytes from two holders mixed leave the one that served the tail to be asked again from offset 0. No frame changes.
