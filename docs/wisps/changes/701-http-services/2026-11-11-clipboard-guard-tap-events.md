On the web, the clipboard guard of a contact's app also lets a copy through in a `dblclick` and a `contextmenu` of the person's, and in a trusted `submit` or `change` that the person's input sets off (its default action, before the first task the guard queues at that input), so a form, a checkbox, a double click or the app's own context menu copies on the person's tap ([On the web](../../701-http-services.md#on-the-web)). A `submit` or `change` the app's script fires itself outside an input, or later, still does not.
