A side that takes no picture: a new optional field `vr: 0` on an offer or an answer says its sender takes no picture and sends none. The receiver rebuilds that signal's video section as `a=inactive` (no stream, no SSRC) in place of `a=sendrecv`, so it sends nothing on the video lane, a camera turned on mid-call included. Any other value says nothing. An older app drops the field and sends as before. Until now nothing in a signal could say it, and a video call to a contact that reads no picture uploaded the camera for the whole call.
