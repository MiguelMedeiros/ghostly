Ringing: a newer offer from the contact whose call rings here, with no hang-up between (their app died or reloaded while it rang out, and they call again), takes the ringing call's place. The chat keeps one "Incoming call" line, the newer offer's; the call rings 60 s from when that offer arrived; and Answer answers it, naming its `ts`. Before, the newer offer was dropped and Answer named the older one, which the caller's app no longer had: its call rang out to "No answer" and this side's said Connecting... until it could not connect. An offer no newer than the ringing one is still dropped. No wire identifier or field changed.
