A call that is on, or a group's call joined, keeps the lock screen from coming on by itself: the person talks and touches nothing, and the time the lock waits for counts again from the call's end. Before, a call where only the person's voice went on counted as idle, and the lock came on in the middle of it. A device that slept meanwhile counts its time asleep as before, and Lock Now still locks at once. No signal was added.
