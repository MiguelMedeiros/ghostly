On a call already: a call that rings while this app is on another call (in another chat) offers End and answer or Decline. End and answer hangs up the call that is on as its person would (the contact gets the hang-up `h`, both chats keep the end line), lets its microphone and camera go, then answers. Decline declines as before and the call that is on goes on. The app is never on two calls at once and places no call while one is on. Before, the ring offered Accept, which opened a second call with a second microphone. The web app, the extension and Desktop (its Linux native media too) follow the rule; the headless CLI is unchanged. No wire identifier or field changed.
