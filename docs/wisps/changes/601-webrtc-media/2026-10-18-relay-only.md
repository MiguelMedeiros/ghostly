Relay only: with Hide my IP from contacts on, a call's connection uses the profile's TURN servers alone under the relay policy, and its signals carry relay candidates only, without a related address, on the session and in a `_call` record. With no relay candidate the call ends with "Your relay did not answer", never direct; call media that cannot keep to the relay policy (the Linux Desktop's call engine) places and answers no call while it is on. Receivers need nothing new.
