A call signal on the session is heard when its frame comes, and its 120 second freshness runs on the receiver's clock from then; an answer names the offer it answers (`o`, a new optional field that apps up to 1.0.1 ignore) and is taken for that offer only, and one that names none is taken when it was heard after the caller's offer went out. Before, every signal's `ts` was held against the receiver's clock: a call from a contact whose clock was two minutes off never rang, and the answer of a callee whose clock was behind by longer than it took to answer was dated before the caller's offer and dropped, so the call never connected. `ts` still orders one sender's signals and settles two calls made at once; a signal read from a `_call` record is still judged by its `ts`. An app up to 1.0.1 still drops the signals of a contact two minutes off, and the answer of a callee whose clock is behind its own.
