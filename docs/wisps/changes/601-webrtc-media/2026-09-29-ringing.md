Ringing: an unanswered call now rings for 60 s. The caller then hangs up with an ordinary hang-up and says "No answer"; the side it rang stops ringing on its own 60 s after the offer arrived, sends nothing, and keeps a "Missed call" line, as it now does when the caller hangs up while it rings. Before, an app rang until someone cancelled and never wrote a missed call. No wire identifier or field changed.
