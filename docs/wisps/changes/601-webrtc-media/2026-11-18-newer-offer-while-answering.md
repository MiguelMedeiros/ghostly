Ringing: a newer offer from the contact that arrives after Answer was pressed, before the answer goes out (the microphone or camera asked for, or the answer gathering), is their call as it is while the call rings. Its "Incoming call" line takes the ringing one's place, and the answer answers it, naming its `ts`; one heard while the answer gathered is answered again on a new connection, as "Offering again" does. Before, it was dropped and the answer named the older offer, which the caller's app no longer had: its call rang out to "No answer" and this side's said Connecting... until it could not connect. No wire identifier or field changed.
