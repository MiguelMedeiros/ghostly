Ringing: a caller whose call rang out unanswered now keeps a "Video call, no answer" (or audio) line in the chat, as the side it rang keeps "Missed call". A caller whose ringing call is declined keeps "Video call declined", one who hangs up while it rings keeps "Video call cancelled", and a call ended while it still connects keeps "Video call ended" with no length. Before, all of these left only "Video call started". A line in the local history only: no wire identifier or field changed.
