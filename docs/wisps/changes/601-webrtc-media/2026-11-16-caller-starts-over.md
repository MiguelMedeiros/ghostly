Offering again, On a call already: the contact starting over applies to a call this side answered too. A new offer from the caller on a call answered here whose connection came up (connected or reconnecting) ends that call with its "call ended" line and its length, sends no hang-up, and the new offer rings; before, the offer was dropped and never rang, and the hang-up this side sent once its reconnect timed out read as a decline of the new call on the caller's side. An offer before the connection came up is still the caller offering again on a new connection. No wire identifier or field changed.
