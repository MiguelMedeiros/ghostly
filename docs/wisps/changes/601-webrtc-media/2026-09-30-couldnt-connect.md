Couldn't connect: a connection with no candidate after 4 s is made again, up to three in all, and an answered call still connecting after 45 s ends. The side that ends such a call hangs up with the new optional field `r: "u"` (could not connect), and both sides keep a "Call couldn't connect" line in the chat, as does a call whose connection failed before it connected. Before, the call window closed with no line, and the other side could not tell this from a person hanging up. Older apps read the hang-up as a plain one.
