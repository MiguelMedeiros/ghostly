A call signal dated more than ten minutes ahead of the receiver's clock is not taken, and the engine hands on a signal from a contact only when it is newer than the last one it heard from that contact on the chat. Since a signal on a live session is no longer refused for a `ts` far from the receiver's clock, one dated a day ahead would have become the latest the receiver knew, and every later offer, answer and hang-up of its sender would have been dropped as older until the app started again; and an answer that names no offer (an app up to 1.0.2), sent again on a new session, was taken for a call placed since. Client policy, no wire change.
