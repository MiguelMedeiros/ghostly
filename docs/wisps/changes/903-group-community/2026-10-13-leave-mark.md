A leaver is now heard for what it wrote before its leave only, not for every epoch it was in: the member who commits a leave signs a mark, `group-left`, of the leaver's highest number in each epoch it heard it in, and broadcasts it with the commit; members hand it on before any of the leaver's stored frames, and take a leaver's frame only at or below the mark of its epoch. A copy of the leaver's state still holds those epochs' secrets, and what it signed after the leave was taken and handed on by any member. Without a mark nothing of the leaver is taken, as for a removed member. An older app drops the new frame.
