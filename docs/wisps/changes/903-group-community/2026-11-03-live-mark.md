A hub that hands on at once a message that came live to it (straight from its author, or with another hub's mark) adds a live mark to its copy, `"lv": <its member key>`, outside the author's signature and never kept or stored. A member takes such a message as live only from the member the mark names, for a message dated at most ten minutes before its own clock, so its app can tell the author's clock by how long it took: a member whose clock runs over a minute behind is heard again through hubs. Apps from before neither set nor read the mark, verify a marked frame as any other, and hand it on as it came, which a member of this revision then ignores.
