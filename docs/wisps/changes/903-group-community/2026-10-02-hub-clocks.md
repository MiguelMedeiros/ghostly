Topology: a reader learns each hub's clock from its beacon entry (the entry moved between two readings, or, for the hub that let a newcomer in, the time of the admitting commit) and, when the two clocks are more than 45 seconds apart, reads that hub's time and `since` moved by the difference; what is written back is each entry as its hub dated it. A member dates its lobby request by the hub's clock when the two differ. Before, a member or a hub whose clock was 90 seconds or more from the others' was cut off from the group for as long as the clocks differed.
