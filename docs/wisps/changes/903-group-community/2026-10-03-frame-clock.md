An app dates its community frames by the clock it dates its commits by. The engine dated messages by the wall clock and commits by its own tick clock, and saying a frame again (revision 2026-10-03) compares the two: where they differed, every member let in while the author was behind looked let in after the frame, nothing was said again, and those members never got the line. On headless engines with 12 members and the admin leaving, about one run in seven lost a line this way (simulated time runs minutes ahead of the wall clock); on a device the two clocks differ by under a second. Not covered: two devices whose clocks differ, the author's behind the admitting member's by more than the time between the admission and the frame.
