Two commits after the same commit by its admin, or an admin change on a branch parting before another admin change, halt the group only when their signer is the admin of the tip of the branch a member follows. From someone that branch moved past (an admin who handed the role on, then perhaps left or was removed) such a commit is a branch that drops the hand-over: it is refused, not kept nor passed on, and the group goes on. No change on the wire.
