A member refuses a membership commit at epoch 2048 or later, and an `add`, `rotate` or `link` commit that leaves the chain without room for everyone else to get out (`e + 1 + 2 × (n − 1) ≤ 2048`), whoever signed it. Before, only the committer applied these rules, so an app that did not could fill the chain for every member: no one could then be removed, leave or join, and the stored chain kept growing.
