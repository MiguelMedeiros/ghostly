§ Admissions while the admin is away: a leave counts toward the members' 32 only when its key's admission was signed by someone other than the admin at that point of the branch (the signer of the next `role` after it, if any), not the admin of the newest commit. Before, once the role was handed on (as it is when the admin leaves), the people the former admin let in at the door counted when they left, and 32 of them leaving among the last 256 commits shut every member's door until the new admin's app let people in. A member of this revision takes an `add` that an app from before refuses for that reason; the two keep different branches until the admin's next change. No change on the wire.
