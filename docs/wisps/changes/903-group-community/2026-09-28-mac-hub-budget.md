A community hub on a Mac keeps within its budget of connections (§ Topology; 9xx Group Mesh § Hubs, Budget): its capacity is also what the budget leaves beside its other groups, its 1:1 chats, its edges to the other hubs and its entry sessions. At that capacity it says a load of 48 in the beacon, as a full hub does, and answers no knock, so members and joiners go to another hub; past it, it drops members, those whose edge is not up first. This closes the open decision on a hub's connections on a Mac (#503).
