A member's high-water mark for an author, in its `group-sync`, is the number just below the lowest it lacks there, not the highest it holds. A live frame raised the mark above that author's frames still coming in a catch-up answer, and when the rest of the answer never came (the member's app closed or its edge dropped, or the hub's app closed), every later sync said the member held them: they were lost for good. A gap asked for by three syncs at least five seconds apart that none filled (the stores rolled past it) is given up, so an answer does not hand the frames above it again at every sync. Nothing changes on the wire.
