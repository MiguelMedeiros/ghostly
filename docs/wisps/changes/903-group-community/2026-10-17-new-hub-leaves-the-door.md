A hub of less than a minute that sees no other fresh hub in the beacon, but knows of one (the member that let it in), is not the door until its minute is over or the other hub's entry is listed. The first hub counts itself among those of the first minute by when it became one, listed or not. In a new community whose first hub's beacon entry the relays held back, the member it let in became a hub and took itself for the door alone too: two hubs answered the same knocks, a joiner waited two minutes, and two joiners let in by different hubs forked the chain. Nothing changes on the wire.
