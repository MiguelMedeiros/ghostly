Metadata: the body may carry `call: { sfu: "<https origin>" }`, the node that forwards the group's calls ([602 · Group Calls](../../602-group-calls.md)). Only the admin sets it, like the name and the picture, and a body that renames the group or changes its picture keeps it. An app that does not know the field drops it and shows no call; an admin on such an app who renames the group turns its calls off. Nothing in the chain or the frames changes.
