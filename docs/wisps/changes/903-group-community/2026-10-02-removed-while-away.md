Hubs exchange the group's frames with current members only, and a key the chain took out is no hub and no door for anyone. The member then shows that it was removed, refuses to send, and stops being a hub. Opening the group's link again knocks with the key the app had: someone removed is let in again and keeps its history, and a member is told it is still in.
