A member takes another member's message frames at a pace: up to what one catch-up answer holds at once (264 frames, 1 MiB and 256 KiB of sealed bodies), then 30 frames and 128 KiB every ten seconds. A frame past it is not taken, stored or relayed and comes again in a later sync answer, and a later frame of the same author under the same commit waits for it, so nothing is lost. Before, every valid frame was taken as fast as it came, so anyone with the link could fill every member's storage and every hub's relaying.
