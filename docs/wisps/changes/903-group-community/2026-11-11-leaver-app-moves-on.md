§ Leaving, "A leaver's app that moves on": the app of a member who left keeps what it follows of the group's chain on the device as well (sealed as a group's record is, deleted when the following ends) and takes it up again when it starts within the ten minutes; when the member opens the group's link again meanwhile, its knock waits until the following is done; while still in the roster it says where it is on each edge that comes up and asks its edges what moved its chain, so a missed `role` commit reaches it; and a member holding the admin's own leave request keeps its edge to that admin for up to ten minutes. Before, opening the link again, a restart or closing the app ended the following before the hand-on, and the group kept a departed admin for good. No change on the wire.
