A gap in a member's sequence numbers that an answer to a `group-sync` did not fill counts as gone once the answer is over (five seconds with nothing more from that member), not as a message on its way (§ Messages and catch-up, Hints are not kept); so does a gap the syncs gave up. A hint is never kept or handed again, so a member who was away while someone fetched a photo saw that member listed with a message that had not arrived yet until the commit changed; frames that fell out of every store did the same. And a hint refused by its author's pace (§ One member's pace) holds that author's next frames under the same commit for up to the two minutes after which it is let go: it is never handed again. Nothing changes on the wire.
