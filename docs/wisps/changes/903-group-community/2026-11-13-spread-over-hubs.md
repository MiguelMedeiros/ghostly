A member picks a hub with room by rendezvous hashing weighted by the room each hub has left, not strictly the least loaded one (§ Topology). The members a closing hub carried all pick in the same second. Each took the least loaded hub, so all of them asked one hub, which opened their 12 or 16 edges at once on its relays' budget and brought none up for minutes. Then they gave it up for the next hub, and some stepped up as hubs with nobody on them. Now they split over the hubs left in proportion to their room. A member's pick is the same for the same hubs, so it does not move from hub to hub. Nothing changes on the wire.
