The pin frame kept beside the store is the newest in the order members show the pin live: by its number `n`, then the frame's time (before, by the frame's time alone). A number more than 10 minutes ahead of the member's clock is no longer taken for one: such a frame is not kept, and one kept from before gives way to the next. An admin whose clock ran a day ahead when it pinned kept that pin in place of every unpin or pin after it, for a day; one 9 minutes ahead whose clock was then set right kept it in place of the unpin, whose number is past the pin's and its time before it. Nothing changes on the wire.
