§ Leaving, "A role that reaches a leave": the app of an admin who left, having handed the role to a member whose `group-leave` reached it, follows its chain past the commit of its own leave while that member still holds the role, until a commit moves the role or its ten minutes end. A hub on an older app (1.10.x) commits that leave at once; its app stopped following then and closed its edges, the only way the new admin's hand-on had to the group when the leaver was its hub, and the group kept a departed admin for good. No change on the wire and no chain rule changes.
