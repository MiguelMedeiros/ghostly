A branch holding a `role` to a member whose standing leave of that admission is known on a branch without the `role` loses to one that holds no such `role` (§ Races and forks, rule 0, before the admin's changes): an admin's hand-over on a view from before the leave no longer leaves the group with an admin who is gone. Such a `role` arriving after the admin's next change on the other branch is refused, not a second history. No change on the wire; apps from before follow the hand-over until they update.
