Client policy, no wire change: a group edge's offer or answer that one relay took and another did not is put on that other relay too, as the edge's own request within the groups' allowance (`GROUP_SIGNALING_ALLOWANCE_SHARE`), when its budget held the packet back (until 2026-10-17 a background write, behind community looks and the chats' reserve) and also, once, when it failed it (no answer, a 5xx or a 428), after its network cooldown. Before, a relay that failed it kept the edge's older offer until its next packet, and a member reading that relay answered the old offer or found none. Every packet already went to every relay; with one relay configured nothing changes. The web app, the extension and the CLI; the Desktop's own Pkarr client does not do it.
