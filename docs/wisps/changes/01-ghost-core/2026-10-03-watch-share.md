A group's edges reading for members that went away from a live session and have not shown themselves back (a newer packet that advertises) say so on the request, and take at most a third of a relay's minute together, the member read longest ago first. Before, each edge watched its member every 2 s for 30 s after a goodbye or a drop: a member left alone in a group whose other members went at once spent both relays' minute on them (with the admin's knock poll), and the member back first was read only when the minute freed, 24 to 37 s later. Client rule in the web app, the extension and the CLI, no wire change.
