A group's edges reading for members that went away from a live session and have not shown themselves back (a newer packet that advertises) say so on the request, and take at most a third of a relay's requests together, spread over the minute. Once a group's read finds a relay's minute full, the groups' reads there are spread over the minute for a minute (a sixth in any 10 s), the key read longest ago first. Before, each edge watched its member every 2 s for 30 s after a goodbye or a drop, and a full minute handed its requests out in bursts: a member left alone in a group whose other members went at once (with the admin's knock poll) read nothing for 18 to 44 s at a time, and the member back first waited that long for its edge. Client rule in the web app, the extension and the CLI, no wire change.
