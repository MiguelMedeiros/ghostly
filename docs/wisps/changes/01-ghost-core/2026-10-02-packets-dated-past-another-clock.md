A writer dates a packet by its own clock and past every packet it has put or read under that key, and when a relay answers 409 it reads the packet that relay holds and, if it is dated later, publishes again dated just past it, once; an empty packet an inviter puts under its contact's key is dated a day before the inviter's clock. Before, a packet was dated by its writer's clock alone, and the relays and the DHT keep only the one dated latest: a joiner whose clock was behind its inviter's stayed unread until its clock passed the warm packet's time, and a member whose clock was behind could not write a group's lobby, beacon or knock record. Client rule in the web app, the extension and the CLI, no wire change; the Desktop's own Pkarr client does not date past a later packet yet.
