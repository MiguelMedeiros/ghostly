Client policy, no wire change: `https://pkarr.miguelmedeiros.dev` is a third default Pkarr relay, after the two Pubky relays. It allows 10 requests a minute an address, so a browser client keeps 5 of them (at worst 7 in a minute with every allowance past the limit), its background requests 4 of those, and the Pubky relays keep their 30 and 60. It takes no read turns: a read goes to it when the Pubky relays refuse, fail or are slow, and writes go to it as to every relay. A profile still on the two Pubky relays, the defaults until then, gets the new list; a list of its own is left as it is.
