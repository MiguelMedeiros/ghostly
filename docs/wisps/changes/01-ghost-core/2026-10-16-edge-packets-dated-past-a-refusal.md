A group edge's packet, signed by the edge so members can carry it, that a relay refuses with 409 goes again at once, signed again dated just past the packet that relay holds, up to twice (three puts at most), while it is still the edge's newest; an edge that starts again (its WebRTC gave up) dates its packets past the last one it signed before, and a packet the edge dated is the key's latest for the next publish dated by the relays' transport (the goodbye after the edge stops). Before, such a 409 failed the publish, or left that relay's copy in place when another relay took it: the link tried again 4 s on with its clock's date, refused as long as the relay's copy was later, and an edge started again in the same millisecond lost its new offer to its last one. Client rule in the web app, the extension and the CLI, no wire change; the Desktop's own Pkarr client does not do it yet.
