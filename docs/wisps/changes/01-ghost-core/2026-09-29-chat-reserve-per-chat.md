Client policy, no wire change: each key is read from the relay after the one that answered its last read, so a relay that missed a contact's newest packet answers it at most once in a row; and the reserve a 1:1 chat keeps on the relays (`CHAT_RESERVE`) ends as soon as that chat reads its contact at a slow pace again (it is live), not a minute after its last fast read. A private group's member back after a restart had one edge wait 48 s more for a read the chats' reserve held back after the chats were live.
