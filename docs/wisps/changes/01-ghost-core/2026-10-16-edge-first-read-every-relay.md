A group edge's first read of its member after it starts, or starts again (the read its offer waits on), asks every relay with a request to spare at once and takes the newest packet among the answers, giving a slow relay 1.5 s more once another answered. The extra requests are plain group reads, never on the edges' signaling allowance; the edge's other reads still ask one relay in turn. Before, the first relay to answer won: one that refused the member's newest packet (409) or had not been sent it yet (its budget held the put back) handed over an older offer or a 404, and the edge waited a whole poll for its next read to reach the relay that had the new one. Client rule in the web app, the extension and the CLI, no wire change.
