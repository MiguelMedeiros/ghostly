Client policy, no wire change: while this app's own WebRTC attempts say its network blocks direct connections (`directPath`, a VPN that drops UDP), a 1:1 chat in use whose contact's record lists `webrtc/1` counts as one that can go live no other way, and takes the listener of the chat whose session has gone unused longest, as a chat with a contact that has no WebRTC does. Before, on an app behind such a VPN whose eight listeners were held by chats with contacts that have no WebRTC (they take them first, `nativeStartOrder`), a chat with a contact that has WebRTC went down with the VPN and stayed down while it was on: WebRTC could not connect and it had no listener to go live over Iroh through its relay.
