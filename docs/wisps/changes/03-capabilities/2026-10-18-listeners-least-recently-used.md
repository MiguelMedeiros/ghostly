When every listener is taken: a group on screen, and the one edge a group keeps, take a held listener as the chat on screen does, and a chat in use takes one from a group that has gone unused longer ([902](../../902-group-mesh.md#transports)). A chat or group used (on screen, or a message in it) within the last two minutes, or more lately than the one taking, is not taken from. Before, group links never took a listener and a chat took one only from another chat or a group link with no session. Client policy, no wire change.
