Client policy, no wire change: when every native listener of a transport carries a live session, the 1:1 chat in use (on screen, or a text going or coming in it over the DHT) takes the listener of the 1:1 chat whose session has gone unused longest, once that is at least two minutes, and never one with a call on or a file moving, nor a group link's. Only a chat that can go live no other way does this (no WebRTC here, or none in the contact's record). The chat that gives it up says goodbye on its session, publishes its record with the transport undescribed and goes on over the DHT until it is in use again. A session just opened, and a listener taken this way, are not taken back within the same two minutes. Before, a chat that found all eight listeners live stayed "On DHT · retrying live" for as long as the others did (#1006: a Linux Desktop or a CLI bot talking to more than eight contacts).
