A reader no longer refuses a capability record for its `issued`: `rev`, which the reader keeps, is what orders records and stops an older one from taking a newer one's place, and a record dated more than a minute past the reader's clock was refused without a word, so a contact whose clock runs two minutes fast (as real ones do) never had its record read and could not be dialled over a native transport before a session. An author now dates `issued` ten minutes before its own clock, so that apps up to 1.0.1, which keep the one-minute rule, take a record from a clock up to about eleven minutes ahead. No wire change.
