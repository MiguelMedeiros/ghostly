The Tasks board: one page with every task card of the profile, from all its chats and groups, in columns by status (Queued, Running, Blocked, Done, and Stopped for failed and cancelled tasks), each with its count, under a line saying how many are active. A card shows its title, its sender, its chat, its pull request and how long ago it changed, and opens its chat on its message; the board can be grouped by bot or by chat and filtered by text, keeps finished tasks for a day, lists routines on their own tab, and is tabs on a phone. It is read only, and it is reached from a line above the chat list that shows only while the profile has a card. No change on the wire.
