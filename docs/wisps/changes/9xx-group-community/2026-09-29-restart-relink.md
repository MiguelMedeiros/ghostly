A member back after its app restarted is in the group again within seconds. A hub whose edge was up and dropped gets the same 20 seconds as a hub that has not opened its edge yet, and up to a minute once it is back, with the edge kept open, instead of being avoided at once; an app that starts again keeps the edges of its last run open for a minute whatever its first reading of the beacon picks; and an edge that was live when the app quit is dialled at once (100 Back after a restart). Measured with three CLI daemons on the public relays, from the restarted daemon's start to the text sent while it was down: a member restarting 37, 31 and 2 s before, 6, 12 and 2 s after; a hub with a member restarting 33 to 36 s in half the runs before, 5 to 16 s after; all three restarting at once about two minutes before, about 20 s after.
