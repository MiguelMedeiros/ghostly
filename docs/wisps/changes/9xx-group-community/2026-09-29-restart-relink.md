A member back after its app restarted is in the group again within seconds. A hub whose edge was up and dropped gets the same 20 seconds as a hub that has not opened its edge yet, with the edge kept open, instead of being avoided at once; an app that starts again keeps the edges of its last run open for a minute whatever its first reading of the beacon picks; and an edge that was live when the app quit is dialled at once (100 Back after a restart). Measured with three CLI daemons on the public relays, from the restarted daemon's start to the text sent while it was down: see the pull request.
