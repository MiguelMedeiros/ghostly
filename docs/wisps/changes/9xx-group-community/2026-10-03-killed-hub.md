A hub whose app is killed (no leave request, no goodbye) cost its members a minute and more. Their side of the edge dialled it again at once, and that connection under way, from their side alone, was read as the hub setting the edge up: they waited a minute for it, not the 20 seconds of a hub that dropped. Then they asked a hub left, which read its lobby every thirty seconds, and their side of the new edge looked for it at the background pace. Now a hub whose edge was up gets the 20 seconds (a minute only once it is back), a member that gives up on it opens its edge to the next hub looking fast for it, as after a leave, and a hub whose edge to another hub drops polls its lobby every six seconds for a minute.
