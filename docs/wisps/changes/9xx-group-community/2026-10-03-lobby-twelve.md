A hub's lobby holds twelve requests, not six, written newest first. When a hub left, the members it carried asked a hub left at once, and with seven the first to write was pushed out and asked again only at its next refresh, 20 seconds later. Twelve requests are 472 bytes sealed, within one Pkarr packet. An app from before reads the first six entries, now the newest six; what it writes (its own request and five others, oldest first) is read the same, and cuts that lobby back to six until the members it left out refresh theirs.
