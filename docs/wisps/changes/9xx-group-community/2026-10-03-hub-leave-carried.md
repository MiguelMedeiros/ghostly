A hub whose signed leave request an app holds is no hub for that app, before the commit too: not picked, not counted, not dialled, and left out of the beacon when that app writes it. A leaving hub takes itself out of the beacon with a write that goes first. A member cut off by its hub's leave offers itself as a hub 15 seconds after the others, so that a member already connected to a hub left behind steps up first, and a member that took a hub in place of the leaver moves once to a lighter one listed since. Two hubs where one is new open their edge expecting each other, and a new hub reads its lobby every six seconds for its first minute. Before, the leaver's members asked the only hub listed, often one whose relays' budget its own members had spent, waited a minute for it, and became hubs that reached nobody: in five daemons on local relays the admin's leave cut its members off for 39 to 63 s and their lines reached everyone after 63 to 73 s. On the headless engines with relay costs, five members and the admin leaving 20 s after the last join, lines reached everyone after 14 to 35 s (median 29), where it was 5 to 157 s (median 107) before.
