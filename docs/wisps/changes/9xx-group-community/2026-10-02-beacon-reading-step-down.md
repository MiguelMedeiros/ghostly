A reading of the beacon that did not happen (the relays held it back) is no longer an empty beacon: the last reading stands, nobody becomes a hub or steps down on it, and a hub writes the beacon only over a reading of that moment. An idle hub steps down in two steps: out of the beacon first, and a member only once its next reading still lists a hub it has an edge up to; otherwise it stays a hub and tries again after a random wait. A hub counts as its member whoever has an edge up to it and is not listed as a hub. Before, a member whose reading was held back became a hub and wrote a beacon naming itself alone, and two hubs elected in the same seconds stepped down in the same second, each onto the other: in both cases the group stayed in parts that did not hear each other, 194 s in a five-member community on local relays after its admin left, and more than 300 s in an earlier run.
