A reading of the beacon that failed is no longer taken for an empty beacon. What the last reading said stays, the reading is tried again five seconds later, and an app that has not read the beacon once since it started is no hub and no door until a reading works or 90 seconds have passed; a hub does not republish the beacon without the reading it merges with. Before, a member whose app started while the relays' budget was spent (every chat and group signaling at once) or while the network was down read "no hubs": it became a hub at once and the door alone, answered the knocks the real door was answering (two hubs on one entry session, and the joiner got in through neither), and its own beacon write, merged with nothing, erased the other hubs for up to half a minute.
