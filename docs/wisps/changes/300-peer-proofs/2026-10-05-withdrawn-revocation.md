A withdrawn proof is also looked up for its revocation, every 12 hours until it expires and a few times shortly after the `idp-withdraw` arrives. Before, only a proof still verified was looked up, so a proof its owner removed while the contact was live stayed "withdrawn" on the contact's side for good, while a manual re-check (the CLI's `identity recheck`) reached "revoked". Removing a proof withdraws and revokes it, and the contact now shows revoked either way. The chat's stop entry for the withdrawal then says revoked, in place, rather than adding a second one.
