A presentation still being checked when its owner withdraws the proof and then shares it again no longer overrides the newer share. Before, the older check's outcome was stored withdrawn over the copy the newer share had just verified, so the contact ended on "No longer shared" while the owner saw it accepted, and a late failure of the older check settled the newer share as refused. Now the verifier drops the older outcome when the proof was shared again since (another challenge of it, or a copy of it stored and not withdrawn), and a check whose proof was withdrawn sends no `idp-result` at all. Nothing changes on the wire; an older app keeps the old behaviour on its side.
