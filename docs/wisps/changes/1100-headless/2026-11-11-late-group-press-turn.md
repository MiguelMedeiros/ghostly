A group press learned after its reply's `group.message` (a copy another member handed on without its reply box, completed by the author's) now carries `message`, the reply as `group.message` gives it, on its `button.pressed`, and `listen --turns` makes that event the agent turn the first one did not start, with `source: "button.pressed"` and the turn's `press`. Before, that `group.message` pressed nothing, so it started no turn, and `--turns` dropped the `button.pressed`: a bot that asked the group never saw the member's answer. When a mention of the profile is learned with the press, `group.mentioned` carries both and is the one turn; the `button.pressed` then carries no reply.
