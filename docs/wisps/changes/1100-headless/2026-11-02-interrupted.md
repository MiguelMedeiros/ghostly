Ctrl-C (SIGINT) during a command that runs through `ghostly` (a daemon's client or a one-shot) now ends it with exit 130 and the `interrupted` error on stdout, a one-shot closing its profile first (its `daemon.lock` released), and one line on stderr saying what became of what the command waited on (`send --wait`, `chat wait`, `file wait`: the message is kept and still goes). On a terminal, a wait that goes on for a second says on stderr what it waits for. Before, the command was killed by the signal: nothing printed, and a one-shot left its lock behind. `daemon` and `listen` stop as before (exit 0).
