The daemon now speaks first on its socket: before it reads anything, it writes a greeting that proves, with the secret in `daemon.secret`, its pid (the profile's lock holder) and the time, and the CLI writes nothing at all, not even the `daemon.hello`, until that greeting checks out. On Windows a process with `SeImpersonatePrivilege` that opened the daemon's named pipe first could impersonate the client from the first line it read; a client that writes nothing to it cannot be impersonated. The daemon also removes `daemon.secret` when it stops, so a greeting copied from it proves nothing afterwards.
