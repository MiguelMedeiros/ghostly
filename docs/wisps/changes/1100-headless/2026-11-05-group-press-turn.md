`listen --turns` makes a group message that presses a button of a question the profile sent (the reply this side's engine marks with `press`) an agent turn, as a mention is: before, a group press named nobody, so it started no turn and its `button.pressed` was dropped too. A press on another member's question starts none. A turn's `press` carries `by`, who pressed, as `button.pressed` gives it: the chat, or the member's key in a group (null when the message names none).
