A contact's app opened with `service open` has a host name of its own, `http://<random>.localhost:<port>/`, answers no other `Host`, and serves only a browser that followed the link `service open` gives (it sets an HttpOnly cookie, then goes on to `/`); the contact's `Set-Cookie` loses its `Domain` and its answer streams through, a cut one ending the response. The daemon makes the profile folder 0700 again before it listens, and `listen` passes an event whose step fails instead of stopping.
