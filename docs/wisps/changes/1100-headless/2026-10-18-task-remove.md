`task.remove` and `routine.remove` (`ghostly task remove <chat|group> <task>`, `ghostly routine remove <chat|group> <routine>`) take back a card of the bot's own: every message of it in that chat or group is edited into a removed card (WISP 405 § Removing a card). `ghostly task remove --all [<chat|group>]` lists every task and routine card the bot sent, and with `--yes` takes them all back, saying each card that refused.
