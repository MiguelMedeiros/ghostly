A contact's call that comes within 2 s of its last one that ended unconnected less than 1 s after it came, or of one held back so, is held back as the apps hold it: nothing rings, no event goes out and auto-answer does not answer it; `call start` in that chat while it may still ring there rings it here instead and is refused as `busy`. An answered call that has not connected answers the contact's newer offer on a new connection up to 4 times, as the apps start over; later ones are ignored, and the call ends as `failed` when its 30 s run out. Before, every such call rang (and auto-answer answered it), and every newer offer was answered.
