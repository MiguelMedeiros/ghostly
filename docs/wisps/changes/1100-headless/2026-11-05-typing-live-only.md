The events `typing.started`, `typing.stopped`, `group.typing.started` and `group.typing.stopped` are live only, as `app.*` are: they never enter the event journal, so `--since` and `--cursor` never replay them, and they take no `seq` of their own but carry the one of the last event before them. A typing state lasts seconds, yet each start, change and stop was journaled: a contact whose status kept changing at the rate the receiver allows turned the 10,000 events over in about 3.5 hours, and a replay handed a bot typing starts from hours before.
