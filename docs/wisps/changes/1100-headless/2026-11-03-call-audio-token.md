A call's audio contract now has a token: `audio` in `call.start`, `call.answer`, `call.list`, `call.get`, `call.outgoing` and `call.connected` carries `token`, 32 random bytes as hex, new for every call. A program sends it and a line feed as its first bytes on the call's socket. On Windows, whose named pipe any account of the machine may open, it is required: until a connection has sent it, the connection gets none of the call's audio and does not replace the program, and one that sends anything else, or nothing for 5 s, is closed. On a Unix socket, owner-only, it stays optional: a program that does not send it works as before. `call pipe` and the `call-echo` example send it. Before, the first connection on the socket was the program, and every new one replaced it.
