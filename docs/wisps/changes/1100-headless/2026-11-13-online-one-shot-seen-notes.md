§ Event stream: a one-shot that runs online (`message send`, `react`, `group send`, ...) reads the seen set's notes of a chat or group only once that history changes while it runs, and then reports what changed in it as a start does (its newest messages checked against the notes, or its whole history). A message that arrived in another chat while no process derived events is reported by the next start that reads that chat's notes: the daemon's, or a one-shot that changes its history.
