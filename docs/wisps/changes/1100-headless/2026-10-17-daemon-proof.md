The CLI now checks that what answers on the profile's socket is the profile's daemon before it sends a request: the daemon writes a new secret to `daemon.secret` in the profile's folder at every start, and answers a `daemon.hello` nonce with an HMAC proof over it; with no matching proof the CLI sends nothing more and refuses with `refused`. On Windows the socket is a named pipe whose name another account of the machine could open first, while the daemon was down, and then received every command's request. `daemon stop` and `daemon restart` stop a daemon of an older release, which cannot answer the hello, as the process that holds the profile.
