`profile backup --light` leaves out files over 1 MiB (voice messages over 4 MiB), as the app's light backup does (WISP 05). The bundle's first record names the files it left out; the restored profile marks their records the first time it starts, and `file save` of one says it is not in this backup. `file save` of a file whose bytes are not on the device refuses instead of writing an empty file.
