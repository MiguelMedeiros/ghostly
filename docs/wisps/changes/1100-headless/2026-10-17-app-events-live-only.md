The events `app.opened`, `app.closed` and `app.message` (Apps as a bot) are live only: they never enter the event journal, so `--since` and `--cursor` never replay them, and they take no `seq` of their own but carry the one of the last event before them. A contact's app sending at its allowed rate filled the journal's byte cap in about 20 seconds and pushed every other chat's events out of it.
