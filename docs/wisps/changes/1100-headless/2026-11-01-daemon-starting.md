A daemon now writes its pid to `daemon.starting` in the profile's folder from the moment it takes the profile's lock until its socket listens, and removes it then (or when it fails to start). `ghostly daemon status` reads it: while the lock's holder is the daemon that marked its start, it answers `{"running": false, "starting": true, "pid": <pid>, ...}`. Before, a daemon still starting its engine was answered `"running": false` like no daemon at all. A mark whose pid is not the lock's holder (a daemon that died while starting) counts for nothing.
