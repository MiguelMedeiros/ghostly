Calls: the CLI follows WISP 601's "Ringing" for a contact who calls again while their call rings here. Their newer offer ends the ringing call as `missed`, with nothing sent, and rings as a new call (`call.incoming`, a new call id), which `call answer` answers. Before, the offer was dropped and `call answer` answered the older one, which the contact's app no longer had: their call rang out to "No answer" and this side's ended `failed`. An offer no newer than the ringing one is still dropped. No wire identifier, field or event reason was added.
