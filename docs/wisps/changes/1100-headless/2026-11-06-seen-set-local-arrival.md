The seen set's 30 days are counted by this device's clock: a message came when its row was stored here, a group's line when this device stored it (the engine keeps that beside the line's commit time), and an edit when it was taken here (kept beside the time its author's clock gave it). A group's line read more than 30 days after its commit, and an edit of an old message from a contact whose clock is a month behind, were taken for old history and never reported.
