A daemon given `GHOSTLY_CALL_SOCKET_DIR` (an absolute path of at most 82 bytes, on Linux and macOS) puts each call's socket at `<that folder>/<call>.sock`, 0660, for a program of another user in the folder's group, and requires the call's token there as on Windows. The folder is the machine's: the daemon does not make it or change its mode, and refuses to start when it is missing, a link, another user's, or when anyone else may write it or anyone outside its group enter it (0750). Without it, nothing changes.
