Calls: the CLI follows WISP 601's "Offering again" for the contact starting over. A new offer from the contact on a connected call the CLI placed, later than their answer, ends that call as `remote-hangup` with no hang-up sent, and the new call rings as `call.incoming` (auto-answer applies as for any call). Before, the offer was dropped and never rang, and the hang-up the CLI sent once the dead path failed read as a decline of the new call on the contact's side. No wire identifier, field or event reason was added.
