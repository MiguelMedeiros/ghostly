A group payment note's line in `group history` (`gpay:<id>`) and in group events now carries `groupPay`: `id`, `kind` (`request` or `payment`), `from` (the payer's member key, or `*` for a request any member may pay once), `to` (the payee's), `by` (who paid it, null when nobody can tell, as for a request to the group paid by invoice), `claims` (the members who said they paid it), `amount` (a string, in the unit's smallest denomination), `decimals`, `unit`, `rail`, `state` (`open`, `sent`, `paid` or `closed`) and `test`. The payment's own line on this profile that the note is about (the request this profile made, a request that came to it over its edge, a payment answering it), as the app tells them, carries `twinOf`, the note line's id: the same payment, which the app draws once. Both lines stay in the output, with their text unchanged. Before, both lines said only the note's text ("⚡ Requested 50 test sats"), which never changes, so a script could not tell whether a request was open, paid or closed, who paid it, or that the two lines were one payment.
