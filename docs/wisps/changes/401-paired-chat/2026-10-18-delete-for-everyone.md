`paired-edit` may carry `"rm": true`: the author deletes the message for everyone (WISP 400 § Delete for everyone). The frame then has a fallback `m` and no `pv` or `sc`, its `e` may be 5,001 at most, and it names a file's or a voice message's id too. The reader keeps a tombstone, confirms it and every later frame for that id, and takes a message that comes again under that id as the copy already there. A `rm` other than `true` makes the frame malformed. Apps from before show the fallback as the edited text.
