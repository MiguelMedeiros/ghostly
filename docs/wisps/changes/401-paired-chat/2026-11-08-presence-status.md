New § Presence and status: with `status/1` agreed in `paired-capabilities`, each side says on the live session whether its person is active or idle (`p`) and a status they set (`s`: availability `available`, `busy`, `away` or `dnd`, one emoji, a text of at most 60 characters, and when it ends), in one `paired-status` frame that replaces the one before. It never goes on the DHT, in the capability record or in groups. Each profile may stop sharing when it is online (Settings, "Share when I'm online", on by default), and Do not disturb silences that side's own message notices. Specified only: no app sends it yet.
