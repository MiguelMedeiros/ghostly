§ Wake-up push: the encrypted body of every wake-up (a message's, a call's, a device's) is one record padded with zeros after the delimiter to 128 bytes (RFC 8188), so all three have one length on the wire; a receiver still reads an unpadded record. § Privacy says what the push service and a relay can still tell: a call or a device handoff from a message, by the `TTL` header and by a call's repeats every 30 seconds.
