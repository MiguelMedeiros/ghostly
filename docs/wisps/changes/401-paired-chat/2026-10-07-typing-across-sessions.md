§ Typing says what a writer does when a session ends while its `start` stands (a transport switch, or a reconnect): it says the `start` again once the next session has `typing/1` on both sides, when the person's last keystroke is less than 6 seconds old, and its rate window carries on across sessions as the reader's does. Before, the contact's indicator went off at a switch and stayed off until the next keystroke, and a `start` said between the two sessions was lost. The frame is unchanged.
