Client policy, no wire change: once the inviter pins the first joiner (on the DHT or on a stream), its envelope sealed to that joiner replaces its first one in the invite mailbox at once, instead of with the next control envelope up to 4 minutes later (or the next read of a live chat, 5 minutes). A later joiner of the same invite reads it on its first look and is told the invite was taken within seconds, instead of pinning the inviter on the first envelope, showing "on the DHT" and its first text "sent" for about 5 minutes.
