A proposal with nothing built, for the owner's review: an encrypted profile, opt-in per profile. What it protects (storage read while the client is not running, a standby's frozen copy) and what it does not (a running unlocked client, memory, copies written before it was turned on); a random data key per profile wrapped under a passphrase of 12 characters or more (PBKDF2-SHA256 600,000, the backup envelope's shape as `ghostly-profile-key`); sealed fields of version 2 under the data key, bound to their database, store, record and field; the secrets of phase 1 and the content left for phase 2; the start gate before any connection, "Lock and stop", migration on and off with a crash-safe mark, a passphrase change that rewraps only the key, no recovery for a forgotten passphrase; backups carrying the data decrypted inside their own envelope; the CLI's unlock; the slices of the build and the open decisions (Encrypted profile).
