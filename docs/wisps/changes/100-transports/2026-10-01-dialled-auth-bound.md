Client policy, no wire change: a native connection this side dialled on a pinned chat must authenticate within 15 s, as one dialled in already must, and the chat dials again when it does not. Found on the Linux Desktop with the web app restarted first: the Desktop's first dial after its own restart opened and then carried nothing, the web app refused it after 15 s, and the Desktop held it until Iroh's idle timeout and dialled again only then (live 34.6 s after it started, against 5 to 14 s the other ways round). A native connection that closes before it authenticated is dialled again at once by the side whose turn it is, and one this side dialled that a fresh capability record shows went to an old address is dropped for the new one.
