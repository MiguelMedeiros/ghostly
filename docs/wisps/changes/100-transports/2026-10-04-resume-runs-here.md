A chat resumes, after a start, only on a transport this app runs. After a handoff the profile last ran on another device, whose connection history moved with it, and the new active device resumed on that device's transport even when it could not start it (a web page taking the profile back from the Desktop on Linux, live on HyperDHT): every dial waited its resume time, the knock never came, and contact offers were held against the other device's session. It now resumes on the first transport it runs that the contact runs too, with no floor, and not at all when they share none.
