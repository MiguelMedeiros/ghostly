A WebRTC connection gone `disconnected` reads the contact's packet at the signaling pace for as long as it stays so, not once ([Back after a restart](../../100-transports.md#back-after-a-restart-revision-06), point 5). A contact whose app crashed and came back after that one read (a tab opened again by hand, seconds later) had its offer read only when the connection was given up, 12 s after it went `disconnected`. Two browser pages, the crashed one loaded again 7 s after the crash: live 11 s after the reload before, about 3 s after. At most six more reads, on the few seconds a connection stays `disconnected`.
