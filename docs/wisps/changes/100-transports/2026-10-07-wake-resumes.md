An app back in front from the background, its session gone, resumes as a restarted one does ([Back after a restart](../../100-transports.md#back-after-a-restart-revision-06), point 6): it dials once whatever its key and knocks on the contact's other transports as its offer goes out, whatever the contact's newest packet, on every return. A session held at the return that does not answer a ping within 4 s is let go first. An iPhone's installed web app with the CLI, away 5 minutes, was live again 59 to 71 s after the return; now 0.4 to 2.2 s.
