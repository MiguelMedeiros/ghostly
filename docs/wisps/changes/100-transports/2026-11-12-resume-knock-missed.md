Back after a restart ([Back after a restart](../../100-transports.md#back-after-a-restart-revision-06), point 3): a native resume dial that does not connect leaves the next dial the resume's, made whatever the key, and that dial knocks on the contact's other transports (relayed ones too) as its WebRTC offer goes out, and on each that comes up while the offer stands. A contact still holding the old session over HyperDHT announces no listener for it, so after a kill or a crash the restarted app's knock always found nothing (PEER_NOT_FOUND), and the app with the higher key, which counted that knock as the resume's one dial, waited for the contact to notice the dead session. Two CLI daemons on a loopback lab (HyperDHT, Iroh through a local relay, WebRTC), the app killed and back 3 s later, 6 chats: 1 or 2 chats of 6 live again 14.5 to 15.2 s after the start before, every chat within 4.4 s after (n=6). No wire change.
