Automatic now connects over Iroh first, then WebRTC, then HyperDHT, on every app where Iroh runs (Miguel, 2026-11-01: "at least the web app and the PWA"). The reason is the time back after the app was away: an Iroh session through its relay comes back with one dial that asks nothing of the relays, where WebRTC needs an offer read and answered through them. Every app ranks the same way, so the rank sum stays symmetric, and Iroh through its relay no longer takes the relayed penalty (a browser's Iroh is never anything else; a relayed HyperDHT still does). Iroh through a relay is slow for bytes, so where both apps list `upgrade/1` the session then moves on itself to the first direct transport ranked (WebRTC between browsers), not only to one ranked first. On Automatic, a resume over WebRTC resumes on Iroh when Iroh ranks ahead. An explicit choice, a contact without Iroh and an Iroh endpoint not up yet keep the WebRTC-first rules. Two Chromium pages and a local relay, one page's process stopped and shown again: live again 0.32 to 0.87 s after 30 s, 2 and 10 minutes away, against 0.13 to 0.38 s (30 s) and 4.3 s (2 minutes) before; a 20 MiB file 0.14 MiB/s through the relay against 14.7 MiB/s over WebRTC.
