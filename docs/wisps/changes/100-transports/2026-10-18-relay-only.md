New section, Relay only: Hide my IP from contacts (`relayOnly`) keeps every path to a contact on relays. It needs a TURN server with its credential; WebRTC to contacts goes through it alone under the relay policy, with relay candidates only; the app's own direct Iroh and HyperDHT endpoints are not offered to contacts; a relay that does not answer fails the call or leaves the chat on the DHT floor, never a direct path; chats start over when it is turned on or off. A profile's own devices keep their transports. No wire change. The engine builds the WebRTC part first; the native transports and the setting in Settings come later.
