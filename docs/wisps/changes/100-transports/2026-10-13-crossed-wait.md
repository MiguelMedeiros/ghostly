Two dials crossing: the lower key's side takes the contact's connection when its own has not connected within half a second, not 3 s ([Back after a restart](../../100-transports.md#back-after-a-restart-revision-06), point 4). A HyperDHT dial that opened and carried nothing is dialled again only 2 s later, and waiting for it held a working connection of the contact's back: in DHT-direct first pairings in a lab, 5 of 20 went live 0.9 to 1.9 s after the contact's dial connected; in process, live 2.4 s after the contact dialled before, 1.15 s now. Two dials that both go through connect within a round trip of each other, and still settle on the lower key's.
