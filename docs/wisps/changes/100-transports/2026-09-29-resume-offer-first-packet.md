A chat or edge that resumes after a restart publishes its first packet after its first look at the contact. Where it offers over WebRTC (a group's edge always does), the offer is in that packet, rather than a presence packet and then the offer: one request a relay per link at start instead of two (with two groups and two chats, 14 requests a relay at start became 8). A resume over a native transport knocks there, and its packet says it is back. No wire change: the packet is the one the offer went in before.
