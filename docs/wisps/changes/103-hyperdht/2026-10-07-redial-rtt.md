The wait before a HyperDHT dial that opened and carried nothing is made again now follows the stream's measured round trip: three times UDX's smoothed RTT, or, before the stream has its first RTT sample, twice the time the dial took to open, never under 2 seconds and never over 8. A fixed 2 seconds closed a stream on a 3-second path before its preface could come, so it dialled again for nothing. Paths under about 0.7 seconds keep the 2 seconds. The wire is unchanged.
