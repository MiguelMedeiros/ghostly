An offer that has its host candidates waits at most 2 s for a server reflexive candidate, not 5 s, and the data link keeps collecting 150 ms after it, not 400 ms. Where the STUN servers never answered, every first-pairing offer waited the full 5 s (One's e2e runs: 13 of 89 offers, plus 9 at 1.3-3 s), and the pairing was live 6.5 to 11.9 s after the join; it is now live in 3.7 s. The trade-off: an offer without a public candidate does not connect to a contact behind an address-restricted (restricted cone) NAT. When such an offer was answered and no path connected, the chat's next offer waits for STUN the full 5 s, so the retry carries the candidate. In fake time, with STUN answering after 3 s: live on the second attempt at 10.6 s instead of 4.45 s on the first.
