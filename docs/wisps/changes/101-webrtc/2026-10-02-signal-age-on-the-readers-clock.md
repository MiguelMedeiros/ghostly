The age of an offer is counted on the reader's clock: it is at most as old as the time since the reader last read the record without it (or it came on a live session just now), and its own `ts` counts only inside that span; an answer has no age to check, since it names the offer it answers, which the reader made in the attempt under way. Before, every signal whose `ts` was more than 120 seconds from the reader's clock, either way, was dropped without a word, so two devices whose clocks were two minutes apart never connected over WebRTC. `ts` still orders one side's signals, and an offer the first read of a run finds is still judged by its `ts`. An answer made again is timed from when its offer was taken, not from the offer's `ts`. Client policy, no wire change; an app up to 1.0.2 still drops the signals of a contact two minutes from it.
