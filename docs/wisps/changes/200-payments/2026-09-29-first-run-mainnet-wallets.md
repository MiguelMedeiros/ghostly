First run: a new profile gets its default Mainnet wallets by itself, in the background and once per profile (Cashu and USDT; Bitcoin on-chain once a Mainnet on-chain wallet can be made without a form). A kind made is never made again, a removed wallet stays removed, and a kind that failed shows why on the wallet page and is retried at the next start. Never in the headless client or under test. "Start on Mainnet" makes the same wallets.
