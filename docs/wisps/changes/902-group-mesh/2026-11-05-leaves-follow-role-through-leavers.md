§ Admission and departure, "A role that reaches a leave": a tombstone the role reached, or went on from in the same step as its leave, keeps edges to every member of its roster (past the size where hubs start, to its hubs) until it goes, and takes a `group-leave` from any of them. One that comes after it handed the role on is answered with the commits of its chain since its leave (at most 16), so that leave follows the role on, and that tombstone stays two minutes after the commit removing it, for the leaves still on their way to it. Before, it kept only its edge to the admin its own leave was owed to: when the admin, its successor and a third member left at once, or the admin made a member the admin while that member and another left, the leave that followed the role to a leaver was lost with that leaver's tombstone, and its member stayed in the roster for good. No wire format change.
