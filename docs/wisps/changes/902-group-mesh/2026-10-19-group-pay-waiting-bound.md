§ Payments: a device keeps at most 256 `group-pay` notes per group that it knows only from someone other than the member they are about, in memory, and drops the oldest first. Before, nothing bounded them: a member could send any number of fresh notes in another member's name, none shown, each kept until the app restarted. A note dropped this way loses only the claims it held; the member it is about still describes it. Client policy, no wire change.
