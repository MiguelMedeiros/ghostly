§ Catch-up says what the receiver does when answers race: each author's messages keep the order that author wrote them ([400](../../400-chat.md) requirement 10, revision 2026-10-12). A message of a member whose later ones were placed in the last three minutes goes above them, which are placed again below it, in the order written. The answer from the author's own log (its newest 32) and another member's hand-on of the older ones are each in order alone, but came interleaved, or the newest first; a member back after 200 messages of one author now reads 1 to 200, the newest last. Nothing on the wire changes, and an app from before still shows such an author's lines as they arrive.
