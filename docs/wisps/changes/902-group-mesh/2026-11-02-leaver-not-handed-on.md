§ Catch-up: a member who left is treated as one removed again. Once a member knows of the leave, it drops the leaver's frames from its log of others' frames, hands none of them on and takes none from another member, whether the admin marked the removal `left` or not. The rule of revision 2026-10-13 believed any frame of the leaver for an epoch before its leave from a third member, and a leaver keeps its seed and the secrets of those epochs: a copy of its state could sign new lines afterwards, dated as it liked, and every member took and handed them on. The cost: a member away when someone left does not get that member's last messages from the others. The `left` mark is still sent and kept, and changes only how the departure is shown. Client rule, no wire change; an older app still hands such frames on, and a newer one refuses them.
