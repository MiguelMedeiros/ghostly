§ Messages, "The name record": each member now says its name on its own, in a `group-name` frame signed by its member key (`v`, a version that only goes up; the name and signature sealed under the epoch key), once when its app starts, when its name changes and on each edge that opens. Hubs pass it on the first time they take it, and a sync carries `nt`, a tag of the records its sender holds, so a member whose tag differs is handed every record. A receiver keeps the latest per member and shows its name when it is newer than the last message or edge that said one. Past 16 members a member that never wrote showed as "Member" and its key to every member it had no edge with, in the members list, the @ list and on its reactions. An app from before drops the frame and the field.
