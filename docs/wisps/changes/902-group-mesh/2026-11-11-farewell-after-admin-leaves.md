§ Admission and departure, a removed member that was offline: the admin that removed it goes on telling it after leaving the group. Its tombstone keeps the edges to the members it removed while they were away, with the chain up to each one's `remove` commit, and stays past the new admin's `remove` of the leaver until each of those members is told (the edge closes as before, 15 seconds after the commit first went) or its week ends. Before, the tombstone went as soon as the new admin heard the leave, and such a member was never told: the group stayed active on its device, and what it wrote went nowhere. Client policy, no wire change.
