§ Payments, "The note": a device's notes belong to the membership it said them in. Leaving or deleting the group, or switching to another group under its id, drops what it kept of them with the history; a member again under that id says nothing about its own payments made before that membership began, and says again when an edge opens only notes naming its current key. Before, an open request to the group came back after a restart under the new key, to newcomers or to the other group's members. Client policy, no wire change: older apps still say it again after a restart.
