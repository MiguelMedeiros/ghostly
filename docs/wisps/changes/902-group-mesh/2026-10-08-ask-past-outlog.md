A member whose sync names gaps (`miss`) for a member it can reach also asks another connected member for that one (`ask`), when an edge opens or in the 60-second gossip turn, one member at a time and each member once until the gaps change. Before, only unreachable members were named in `ask`, so a member back in a busy group whose author's edge was up got only that author's last 32 messages, from the author's own log, although the other members held the rest. Nothing changes on the wire.
