§ Transports: native listeners go to the chats and groups in use, and when none is free the one unused longest (not on screen, no message, for two minutes at least and longer than the one taking) gives its own up, 1:1 chats and groups alike. A group not on screen keeps one edge (to a hub or the admin, else one already holding a listener, else the member heard from last) and takes a held listener for it; the group on screen takes them for all its edges, least recently used first. Before, a group's links never took a listener a chat held: an app with eight chats live over native transports (a Mac on a VPN that drops UDP, where WebRTC carries nothing) reached none of its groups' members, "0 of N reachable" for as long as the VPN was on. Client policy, no wire change.
