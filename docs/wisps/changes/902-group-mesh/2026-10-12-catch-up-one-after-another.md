In catch-up, the members whose syncs waited for the first member's answer are now told one at a time: the first of them is sent the app's sync with the others still waiting named in `ask`, those wait for its answer as they did for the first (no message written before the asking for a second and a half, its edge down, or three minutes), and so on down the line. Before, all of them were told together once the first answer had come, and each answered with its own messages alone. When the member asked first had been away too, it held little of what was missed and its answer ended at once, so the conversation came as one member's lines, then the next's: 30 messages three members wrote in turn arrived with 155 pairs out of order, none now. Each answer is one list in the order written (as far as two logs can say), and a member asked for messages the app already has hands on none of them. The cost is time: the last of n members waiting is told about a second and a half times n later. Nothing on the wire changes: `ask` already names reachable members when some of their older messages are missing.
