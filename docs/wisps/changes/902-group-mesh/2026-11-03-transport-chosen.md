§ Transports: a transport chosen for a group. A member may put a group on Iroh, HyperDHT or WebRTC on its own device, as it may a 1:1 chat; Automatic, the default, is the rule from before. Iroh or HyperDHT runs that native endpoint alone on each of the group's edges, with no WebRTC, and the edge's `_tr` lists that transport alone; WebRTC publishes `["webrtc/1"]` from the edge's first packet. No new field: the member's app follows `_tr` as it did, so members who disagree meet on the native transport, and apps from 1.7.4 and 1.8.0 meet a member who chose without any change. Two members who chose different native transports: an app whose member's `_tr` lists neither `webrtc/1` nor its own choice starts the member's native transports too. Entry sessions keep the rule from before.
