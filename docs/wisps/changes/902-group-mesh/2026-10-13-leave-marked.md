The admin's `remove` commit made on a member's own leave (its `group-leave` or signed `group-bye`) now goes with `left`, the admin's signature over `["ghostly-group/1 left", g, e, <commit hash>]`, on the `group-commit` frame. Members keep it with the chain and hand it on with that commit. A removal the admin chose carries none, and a member cannot add one. A `remove` commit alone did not say whether the member asked to leave. Apps from before drop the field, and the chain is unchanged.
