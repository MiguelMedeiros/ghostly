An entry session of a group's link whose WebRTC attempt connected nothing goes on over a native transport, as an edge does: it runs its native endpoints, its `_tr` lists no `webrtc/1`, and the other side follows. Before, entry sessions stayed WebRTC only: a joiner or an admin on a network that lets no direct connection through (a VPN, a firewall) saw the other side's packet and never got the welcome, and the join stayed at "Let in: getting the group's keys". Edges stay native on such a network: when the app's own attempts say its network blocks direct connections, every group link of its still on WebRTC goes native, the edges it only answers on too; and an edge gone native no longer goes back to WebRTC when its member returns once it went live natively, once WebRTC failed again after a return, or while the network blocks. Until the app's network changes or it restarts. Before, an edge whose WebRTC was live and died when a VPN came on could stay down, and a member's packet that left `_svc` out for size put a native edge back on a WebRTC that died again, while the member's 1:1 chats went on over Iroh. No wire format change.
