An invitee keeps at most four invitations waiting from one contact chat; a further one is answered with `group-decline` and not kept, where before only a limit of 32 for all chats together applied and anything past it was dropped unsaid. The `n` and `e` an invitation names are kept within what a group can be (`n` 1 to 32, `e` 0 or more). Nothing changes on the wire; an app from before keeps and shows them as sent.
