§ Hubs, "Presence": a hub's `group-reach` may carry `v`, the members the hubs it hears from list in their own `k` (on edges up with it), but those in its own `k` and itself. A member takes it from a hub of the beacon only, never longer than the roster, and shows those members reachable through that hub; edges, stand-ins and `u` still go by `k` alone. Before, a member on another pair of hubs showed offline though everything it said reached the group through the hubs: in a group of 24 on hubs, an admin whose two hubs were not the others' saw 17 members away. Apps from before ignore `v`; a hub on such an app sends none, and its members show what they did before.
