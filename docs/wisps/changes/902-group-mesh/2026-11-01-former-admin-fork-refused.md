§ Membership chain (Forks), § Security and privacy: a different commit for an epoch already in the chain halts the group only when its signer is the admin of the receiver's tip. From a former admin (one that handed the role on or was taken out) it is a stale branch that drops that change, refused like any other; the admin of the moment signing two histories still halts it. Before, anyone who was ever the admin, the creator after a hand-over included, could halt the group for every member with one commit after an epoch it was the admin of. Client rule, no wire change; an older app still halts on such a commit.
