In catch-up, a member whose edge opens while its own messages were asked of another member under ten seconds ago is sent the app's sync only once that answer has come: no message from the member asked for a second and a half, its edge down, or ten seconds after it was asked. Before, every member whose edge opened was told at once and answered with its own messages, which raced the first member's answer, the one that holds everyone's messages in the order they were written; the receiver places each message where it arrives, so a member back in a group of five read each member's lines apart (30 messages written in turn came with 92 pairs out of order, the newest 14 rows above the last). Nothing on the wire changes, and a sync the other side sends is answered at once.
