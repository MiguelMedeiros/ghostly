§ Hubs: a commit goes on with the receiver's secret. A hub passed every commit on without a secret, and each member that took it from a hub had to ask that hub for the new epoch's secret; when that hub had itself taken the commit from another hub without the secret, the answer was empty and nobody else was asked, and the admin's own copy, arriving after a hub's, was dropped with the secret it carried. Members of a group past 16 then could not write for 15 to 66 seconds after each join, leave or removal, and one for good after a key rotation. Now a hub that holds the secret seals it in the commit for each member it passes it to (`secret`, as in a sync answer), and one that does not passes the commit on once more, sealed, when the secret reaches it; a member takes the secret from any later copy of a commit it already applied, and while it still lacks its newest epoch's secret, asks whoever hands it a copy without one. The frame is the one a sync answer already sends; older apps take the sealed secret from a first copy and drop later copies as before.
