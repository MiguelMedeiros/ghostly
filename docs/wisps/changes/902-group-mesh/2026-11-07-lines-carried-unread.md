§ Catch-up, "Carried unread"; § Hubs, "Passing on": a member not in a message's epoch keeps a whole frame its author signed while in that epoch and in the roster, unread, passes it on as a hub, and hands it at catch-up to a member of that epoch that asks for the author; a member hands a hub it counts on its own messages and the kept frames of the authors the hub asks for of epochs the hub was not in, once each (16 per author at most), and an author sends its own to such a hub once it counts on it. Before, a hub let in after a message dropped it and nobody handed it one, so on hubs that had changed since, lines written while no hub was up reached most members only when the old hub came back.
