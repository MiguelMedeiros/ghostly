§ Edits, "Handed on" and "Carried unread": a member not in a message's epoch (a hub let in after it) keeps the edits of that epoch it carries unread once their author is out of the roster, since it cannot tell a removal from another edit, and hands them at catch-up to the members of that epoch, who take only the removals; a member answering the sync of a hub it counts on hands it the removals of authors out of the roster too, unread. Before, such a hub dropped them, so a member back whose only hub came after the message never got a deletion for everyone once its author had left or been removed.
