In catch-up, what an app asked of one member for another, unreachable one ends when that other member is reached again: its edge opens and its sync goes, at once or after the wait for the first answer. The next time it is unreachable, it is asked for anew, of the member whose edge opens. Before, the ask counted for a minute while the member asked stayed connected, whatever happened to the other one meanwhile: an app that reached everyone, slept and came back within that minute did not ask the first member for that one's messages, which then came from their author alone, apart from everyone else's lines. Nothing on the wire changes.
