§ Transports, The edge each member keeps: in a private group, the one edge a member keeps while the group is not on screen goes to the member admitted just before it (the roster in admission order, from the chain), the first member's to the second and the newest's to the admin, instead of every member's to the admin. The edges kept make a tree over the roster that both ends compute alike, each member on two of them at most; a join changes the edge one member keeps, a leave two at most. An admin with no WebRTC no longer has every member of every group to answer within its eight listeners (ghostly-agents#1509). Communities keep the admin's edge first. No wire change; apps from before still keep the admin's edge, which the admin answers.
