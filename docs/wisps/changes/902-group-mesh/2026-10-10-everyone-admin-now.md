Everyone (`*`, § Mentions) is kept on receipt only when its sender was the admin of the message's epoch and is the admin still, for a message and for an edit; the sender's app drops it from an edit the same way. Before, the admin of the message's epoch was enough, so a member who had handed the role on could still name everyone under an epoch from before, for as long as the members kept that epoch's secret. A message the admin sent just before handing the role on loses its `*` for a member who receives it after. Nothing on the wire changes.
