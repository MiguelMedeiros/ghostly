§ Private messages (in the core, no app sends or takes it yet): a member starts a 1:1 chat with another by handing it a fresh `ghostly1` invite in a `group-pair` frame, sealed to that member alone as a community's pair payload is, on their edge or passed on once by a hub (`h`, only from the edge's own member, at most 10 a minute). The body is a request (`c`, the code; `k`, the participation key it pins; `n`, a name), `got` or `no`. Live only: no log, store or catch-up. One request per member every ten minutes, three a day, none for a day after a `no`, kept by both sides; a muted or blocked member's request is dropped silently. `paired-groups` version 8; with no `got` in 30 seconds, or an edge without 8, the sender shows the invite to copy.
