An app that published `_tr` on a group's link now goes on publishing it there in its later runs, with what it runs then (`["webrtc/1"]` alone when it has WebRTC and runs no native endpoint), and an app forgets what it kept of a member whose packet has advertised with no `_tr` for two minutes. Before, an app whose WebRTC connected nothing on an edge said `["iroh/1"]` for that run and nothing at all in the next, while the member's app kept the last value it read and acted only on a `_tr` that was there: it went on dialling an Iroh endpoint nobody ran, or found no transport in common, and the member stayed unreachable across restarts of both apps. Both rules are additive: a released app replaces what it kept when it reads the `["webrtc/1"]`, and an edge between two apps that never left WebRTC publishes no `_tr`, as before.
