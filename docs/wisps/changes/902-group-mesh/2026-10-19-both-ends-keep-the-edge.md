§ Transports: both ends keep the same edge. Each side picked the one edge a group not on screen keeps on its own, and the picks did not always meet: with no WebRTC, a member of a third group kept its edge to the admin while the admin, its group listeners held by two other groups, gave that edge none, and the member kept no edge at all (ghostly-agents#1437). Now an edge whose member keeps it (its `_tr`, read in this run, lists a native transport this app runs) counts as one its group keeps on this side too, on one transport, past what a group's other edges may hold, so the admin or a hub answers every member that keeps its edge to it. And a member whose kept edge gets no answer in 20 seconds (no session, the other side lists no listener there or is away, no other edge of the group live) keeps the next one instead: hub, then admin, then the member heard from last. Client policy, no wire change.
