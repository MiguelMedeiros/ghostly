§ Edits, "Carried unread"; § Hubs, "Passing on": the author of an edit also sends it to the hubs it counts on that were not in the message's epoch, and a member not in that epoch keeps an edit its author signed while in the roster and passes it on as a hub, unread, and hands it on at catch-up to a member of that epoch that asks for the author. Before, a hub let in after a message dropped its edits and the author sent it none, so on hubs that had changed since, an edit or a deletion for everyone of an older message reached no other member.
