In catch-up, the wait before a member whose messages were asked of another is sent the app's sync now lasts as long as that answer takes: it ends when no message written before the asking (by the time each message carries) has come from the member asked for a second and a half, when its edge is down, or three minutes after it was asked. Before, it ended ten seconds after the asking, and any message from the member asked counted as its answer. An answer goes sixteen frames at a time, the next once the app handled the one before the last, and a phone with a long history handles a frame in hundreds of milliseconds: an answer of 200 messages at four a second was cut at the 40th, the other members answered with their newest 32, and those landed above the older lines still on their way (7072 pairs out of order of 200 messages; none now). Counting only what was written before the asking, a member that goes on writing no longer keeps the others waiting, which is what lets the longest wait cover a whole answer (the 256 messages kept and its own 32, at 600 ms a frame). Nothing on the wire changes.
