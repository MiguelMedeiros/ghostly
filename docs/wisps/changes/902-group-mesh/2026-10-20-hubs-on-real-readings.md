A member steps up as a hub only on readings of the beacon that relays answered, and with a margin: while fewer than six hubs (eight less two) are counted, the beacon's fresh ones together with those a reading showed fresh within the last three minutes, after a delay of its own of up to a minute (none while there are fewer than two), on a reading made since. A hub not in the beacon steps down on the same count. A read the relays' budget held back, which answers the packet already known, is no reading, and a hub writes no beacon merged from one; a write the budget held back is tried again when the budget frees a request (at most 30 s), not every 5 s. Before, on real apps whose reads the budget held, 14 to 20 of 20 members stayed hubs for minutes. No wire format change.
