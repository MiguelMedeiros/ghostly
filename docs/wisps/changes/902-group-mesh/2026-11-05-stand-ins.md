§ Hubs, "Stand-ins"; § Compatibility; § Bounds; § Security and privacy: a hub says in `group-reach` whom neither it nor any hub it hears has reached for 30 seconds (`u`, optional, at most 16). A member that is not a hub takes it only from a hub of the beacon and keeps an edge with each such member it is one of the two stand-ins of (by rendezvous rank, in key order), four at most. A member back behind the chain, whose remembered hubs all left or closed while a commit let a new hub in, dialed only members that never opened their side and reached nobody for good; its stand-ins now open theirs, and it catches up over them and moves to the current hubs. Apps from before ignore `u` and stand in for nobody; a hub on such an app sends none.
