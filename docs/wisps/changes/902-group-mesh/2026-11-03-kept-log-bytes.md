§ Catch-up: the log of other members' frames kept to hand on holds 256 frames and 6 MiB, up from 256 KiB. 256 of the longest texts (16 KiB, about 22 KB each as a frame counts) now fit, so a member back gets the group's last 256 messages whatever their length; only messages carrying a card or a file beside a long text give way before their count. Before, 256 fit only below about 440 ASCII characters a message on average, and one author of long messages left a member who was away with far fewer (149 of 200 at 1,000 characters). The frames are kept with their messages, not in the group's record, so the larger bound costs memory and no larger writes. Client rule, no wire change.
