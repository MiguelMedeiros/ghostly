A `group-edit` whose box holds `{ "text": <fallback>, "rm": true }` deletes the message for everyone (WISP 400 § Delete for everyone): `v` may be 5,001 at most, a file's announcement is named as any message, and the receiver keeps a tombstone so later edits and copies handed on change nothing. A box whose `rm` is not `true` is dropped. The removal is kept, handed on and said again to an edge that opens as the message's latest edit. Older apps show the fallback text.
