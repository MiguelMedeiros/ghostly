A private group's hubs stay within the beacon's eight. A member steps up only on a reading of the current epoch's beacon: a reading the relays' budget held back, or the empty beacon a commit leaves until it is read again, no longer counts as "fewer than eight". And a hub that is not pinned, which a reading no longer shows beside eight other fresh hubs, steps down instead of writing itself in again (which pushed another hub out, and so on in turn). Before, in a group past 16 members whose apps all stay online, a burst of joins (each one a commit) made most of them hubs, and none stepped down: 13 to 19 hubs of 20, each with an edge to every member, so the group was a full mesh plus a beacon. No wire format change.
