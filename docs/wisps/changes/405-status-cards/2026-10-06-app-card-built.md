The `app` kind is built: the reader keeps a card only when its `ref`, `digest`, `sequence`, title and an `id` made from the `ref` hold, the sender refuses what a reader would drop or cut, and the fallback text puts the bundle's URL on a line of its own. Its vectors are `app-card.json`. The web app draws it in a 1:1 chat behind the apps flag; elsewhere, and with the flag off, the message shows its text.
