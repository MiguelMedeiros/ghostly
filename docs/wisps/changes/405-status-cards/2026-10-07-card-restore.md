A card message whose text went on the DHT floor or into a hold now gets its card: once the chat is live with an app that shows the kind (`status-card/1` for a task or a routine, `apps/1` for an app card), the author's app sends it again as an edit of the card alone, the same text, so the reader shows the card on the same message with no version and no edit mark. Once per message, restarts included, for the newest 50 noted messages of the last 7 days. Before, an app card or a task sent once while the contact was away stayed text for good, since no update came to carry its card. No change on the wire.
