A contact's row in the chat list says whether its bot is working, from its task cards: a dot just before the usage meter's place, in the accent with a slow pulse while one of its tasks is `running` and the newest of them was updated in the last 15 minutes, a muted ring, still, once they are all older (stale), and absent when no task is running. Nothing changes on the wire: it is a rule for showing cards ("Showing a card"). A bot that wants to read as working edits its running task at least every 15 minutes.
