A bot removes its own card from every member's device by deleting its message for everyone (WISP 400 § Delete for everyone), numbered one past its last update, so a card at its 5,000 updates can still go; the CLI says it as `task remove <chat|group> <task>` (every message of mine with that task's card there, or `--all`) and `routine remove`. A removed card counts in no Tasks view or working dot. An app that shows cards keeps the card it last took when the removal is numbered past 100.
