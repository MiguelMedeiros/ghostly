Adds the `app` kind, planned with WISP 1200 for release 1.2: a card that a person's app makes by itself when the person shares a mini-app or opens one in a chat, not only bots. It carries `ref`, `digest` and `sequence`, with `title`, an optional `version`, `url` and `opened`, and an `id` made from the reference; it is never updated, counts in no Tasks view, and its one control opens the reader's own install screen, with nothing fetched to show it. Its fallback text names the app and puts the URL on a line of its own, so an app without the kind, or without cards, shows a link. The title becomes Status Cards for Bots and Apps, and the open question of this WISP's number, assigned on 2026-10-05, is gone.
