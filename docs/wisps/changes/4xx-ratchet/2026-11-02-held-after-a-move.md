After a move to another device or a restore, items the contact held in the session it shared with the device before are no longer lost. The reader leaves an item under a session it never had unopened and unacknowledged, instead of refusing it, and reads it again once the manifest is rewritten; the sender seals its held items again in the session it seals with as soon as that changes (the new device's floor envelope made it current), not only on reading a held item of it, and a retry goes after them. No wire change.
