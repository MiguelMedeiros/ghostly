First draft: `ratchet/1`, a double ratchet in the manner of Signal's for the two paths of a 1:1 chat that store what they carry. DHT floor envelopes after the pin (a `_dr` record in place of `_dm`) and held item bundles (version 2) are sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305 under keys used once: an X25519 step each time the turn passes, and a chain of keys per lane between steps. The root comes from the two participation keys and the invite, per session. It sets the 50-byte header, the roles, both sides starting at once, a side that lost its state starting anew (a floor session the contact starts becomes current at its second envelope, so a stale envelope never splits a chat), the bounds on keys stepped over and kept (a sender reaching one starts a new session), one lock per chat over both lanes, the downgrade rule (a lane once heard refuses plain items), and that ratchet state never leaves the device: what went over the DHT or was held cannot be opened again from the seed. It says plainly that a session's first chains open with a participation key, until the turn has passed both ways. Test vectors included. The live session is unchanged: its transports already agree fresh keys per connection.
