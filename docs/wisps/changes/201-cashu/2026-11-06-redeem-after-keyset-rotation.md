A redeem whose outputs the mint refuses for their keyset (retired or unknown after a keyset rotation), with its token unspent, is stored again with new outputs on the mint's current keyset and sent once more, as a paid invoice's outputs already were; a second such refusal ends it in the wallet's own words with the token still unspent. A redeem found stored at a start with nothing signed and its token unspent is sent again, however long the app was closed, and ends unredeemed only on the mint's word that the token is spent or refused.
