Every request that asks a mint to sign new outputs is stored before the mint is asked, with its inputs and its blinded outputs: redeeming a token, splitting ecash for a token or a Lightning payment, and minting the ecash of a paid invoice, as the reviewed payment already was. When an answer does not arrive, the wallet asks the mint for its signatures on those outputs (NUT-09) and the state of the inputs (NUT-07), at once and at every start, then stores the result once or frees the inputs. A contact whose ecash is being redeemed is answered once the mint has spoken.
