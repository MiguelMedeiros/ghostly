- In a group call, set how loud each person plays for you, from 0 to 200%, with the speaker on their tile: their voice and the sound of a screen they share. Only you hear the change, 0 mutes them for you, the slider stops at 100% on a speaker the app can't make louder there, and the app keeps each person's volume until it closes.
