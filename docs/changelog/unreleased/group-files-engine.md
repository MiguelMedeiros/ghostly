section: For users / Groups
---
- Files and voice messages in groups, from the CLI for now (`ghostly file send <group> <path> [--voice]`): everyone in the group sees the file at once, voice messages and files up to 8 MiB download by themselves, and a member who was away gets the file from any member who has it. The app's composer and bubbles come next; until then, and in apps from before, a group shows a line that names the file.
