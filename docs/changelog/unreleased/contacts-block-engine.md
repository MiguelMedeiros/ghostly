section: For developers
---
- The engine keeps a list of blocked contacts in the profile's settings (`setContactBlocked`, and `blocked` on a 1:1 chat's view): what a blocked contact's 1:1 chats bring (messages, files, reactions, pins, edits, payments, call offers) is dropped with nothing shown or rung, and it is told not to wake the app. The chats keep what they had. The CLI command and the app's Block buttons come next.
