section: Fixed / Calls
---
- On a phone with the keyboard up, a call's small window (one-to-one or group) no longer covers the message field or hides behind the keyboard. Held upright it moves above the field; on its side it becomes a one-line bar over the chat's header with the name, the clock, Mute and End call. It goes back to its corner when the keyboard goes down.
