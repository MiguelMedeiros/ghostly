- See when a contact is online: a dot on their picture in the chat list, the chat's header and their panel (green, amber when idle or away, red when busy or Do not disturb), and the status they set, an emoji and a short text, beside their name. A contact who does not share when they are online shows no dot, and a status goes away when it ends.
