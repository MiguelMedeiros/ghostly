section: Fixed / Groups
---
- If you are the admin of several private groups whose links are on, your app no longer checks each link for people joining every 5 seconds on its own. With three links on and nobody joining, that was 36 relay requests a minute, all day, more than all the members of a group of 8 cost. Links nobody is using now share one pace, each checked at most every 15 seconds: three links cost what one did. A link you just shared, or that someone just opened, is still checked every 2 seconds.
