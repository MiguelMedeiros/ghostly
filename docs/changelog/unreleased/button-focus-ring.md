- The buttons most screens use (Start on Testnet, Check now, a wallet's Send and Receive, and the rest) show a ring when the keyboard reaches them, 3:1 or more against the page in every theme, light and dark. Before, they had the browser's own thin ring, which in Chrome, Edge and the Windows and Linux desktop apps could take the page's own colour: on a dark page a filled button's focus showed nothing at all.
