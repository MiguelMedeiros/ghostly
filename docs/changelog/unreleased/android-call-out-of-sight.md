---
section: Fixed / Calls
---
- In the Android app, a call no longer ends by itself when you press Home, switch apps or turn the screen off. Android silenced the microphone a few seconds later and the call dropped after about 45 seconds. While a call is on, the app now shows an ongoing "Call with …" notification: tap it to go back to the call, or use Hang up. The app asks to show notifications once, at your first call, if it never asked before.
