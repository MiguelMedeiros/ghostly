---
section: Fixed / Calls
---
- A video call whose camera is refused or missing (the mic allowed) now starts, or is answered, as a voice call with a short note that the camera is off and why, instead of not ringing at all or hanging up. You can still turn the camera on later in the call. When a call can't use the mic, the note now says whether it was the mic, the camera, or both, instead of "the mic or camera".
