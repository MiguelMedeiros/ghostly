---
section: Fixed / Calls
---
- A video call whose camera is refused or missing (the mic allowed) now starts, or is answered, as a voice call with a short note that the camera is off and why, instead of not ringing at all or hanging up. Its lines in the chat say voice call, as the contact's do. You can still turn the camera on later in the call, and the note goes as it comes on. When a call can't use the mic, the note now says whether it was the mic, the camera, or both, instead of "the mic or camera". In the Android app, a video call asks for the mic and then for the camera, once each, so refusing the mic once never shows its dialog a second time.
