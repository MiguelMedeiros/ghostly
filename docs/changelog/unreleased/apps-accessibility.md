- Behind the apps flag: the accessibility test (axe, WCAG 2.2 A and AA, light and dark, left to right and right to left) covers the Apps screens: the Apps page, Add, the install screen, the chat picker, + > Apps, the app's panel beside the chat and over it on a phone, the app card on both sides, the app's details and Uninstall.
- Behind the apps flag: a screen reader hears an installed app's line (Stopped, an update that asks) with its row, and the focus goes to Add after an app is uninstalled or a store removed, instead of being lost on the page.
