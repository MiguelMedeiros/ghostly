- Notification sounds no longer play on top of one another. When many chats and groups catch up at once (after the app restarts, or while bots post), you hear one sound instead of a pile: messages from all chats that come within 1.5 seconds are one sound, and any other sound waits a second after the last one, with a mention or a payment heard over a plain message that comes with it. A ringing call is never cut short, and nothing else plays while it rings.
