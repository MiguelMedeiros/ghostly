section: For developers

- Sixth part of "one profile on several devices, one active at a time" (WISP 06): the forced takeover and the guard on
  restoring a backup. It is not available to people yet: the work is on a branch. A copy of a profile started from
  older state (a takeover from a standby's frozen copy, or a restored backup) now sends above every number the copy it
  replaced may have used: in a group or a community, its messages are no longer dropped by the other members as
  already seen, which before happened to the first messages of every restored backup.
