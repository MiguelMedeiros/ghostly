- Files take a quarter less of the network: between two apps that have this release, a file's bytes travel as they are instead of as text, so a 200 MB video sends about 200 MB, not 268 MB. This works on direct (WebRTC) connections, on HyperDHT from the CLI and from a browser, and for files in groups. Older apps, Iroh and the Desktop's HyperDHT keep the old way.
