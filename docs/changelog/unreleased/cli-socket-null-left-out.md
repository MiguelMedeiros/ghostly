- A request on the daemon's socket that gives a field as `null` reads it as left out, as it reads every other field. Before, a null was read as false, 0, Testnet, Cashu or Lightning: `call.auto` or `group.auto` with `"on": null` wiped the chats they answer or join from (left out, they only say them), `profile.set` with `"shareProfile": null` stopped sharing the profile, and `chat.disconnect` with `"hold": null` lifted a hold and did not disconnect. Socket answers that change: `wallet.list` with `"network": null` lists every network (Testnet only before); `payment.list` with `"chat": null` lists every payment and `status.set` with `"until": null` sets a status with no end (both refused before); `chat.accept` with `"method": null` says what the chat takes (it turned Cashu on, or off with `"on": false`), and with `"networks": null` keeps the networks (refused before); `pay` and `chat.payRequest` with `"maxFee": null` pay with no ceiling (any fee was refused), and `chat.payRequest` with `"via": null` pays as with `via` left out (over Lightning before); `chat.request` with a null `method` or `rail` asks as with them left out. A switch that is on when left out (`group.link`, `chat.accept`'s `on`) still reads `null` as off.
