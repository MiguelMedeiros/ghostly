- Behind the apps flag: an app opened in a chat keeps Ghostly's look. On a wide screen it opens beside the chat, which stays usable, with Full width and Close. On a phone it covers the chat, with Back to the chat, and the app keeps running until you close it. Its header shows the app, "with" the contact and their picture, and the chat's connection icon. On Desktop, an app opened in a chat gets a window titled after it and the contact, such as "Chess with Ana".
