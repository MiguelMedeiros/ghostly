section: For developers
---
- Tenth part of "one profile on several devices, one active at a time" (WISP 06): the final screens. It is not
  available to people yet: the work is on a branch. Profile, Devices lists each device with a mark for its kind and its
  state (This device · Active, Active, Standby, Not finished), "Move to <device>" on the row and Check and Remove in the
  row's menu, and the details behind ⓘ marks. The standby screen, the handoff progress, the takeover, the remove and
  the lost-or-stolen screens share one layout at phone and desktop widths, in both themes. The account switcher shows
  "Standby" for a profile this device is on standby for. A start that could not check which device is active asks first:
  "Try again" or "Start anyway". Settings says when the push address belongs to another device, and a phone that was
  never the active device shows its quiet notices in the app's language.
