section: For developers / Apps
---
- Behind the apps flag: an app's manifest says where it shows. A chat app (the default, such as Chess) runs only in a chat, so Open on the Apps page asks which chat. A full-screen app opens alone from the Apps page; opened from a chat it covers the chat, with the contact, the connection and Back to the chat. On Desktop its window opens at the Ghostly window's size.
