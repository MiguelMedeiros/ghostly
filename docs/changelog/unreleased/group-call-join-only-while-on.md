- A group's header asks the group's call node whether its call is on: "Join call" (labelled, in the call colour) shows only while someone is in the call, with how many in its tooltip, and a plain phone, "Start call", while nobody is. The app asks only while the group is open and the window shown, at most every 15 seconds, and less and less often while the node gives no answer. A node that doesn't answer it shows "Join call" as before.
