section: For users / Calls
release: 1.14
---
- A group's header asks the group's call node whether its call is on: "Join call" (labelled, in the call colour) shows only while someone is in the call, with how many in its tooltip, and a plain phone, "Start call", while nobody is. The app asks only while the group is open and its window in front, at most every 15 seconds: less and less often (up to 10 minutes) while the node gives no answer, as long as a busy node asks it to wait, and no more at all of a node that can't tell, until the group's call moves to another node. A node that doesn't answer it shows "Join call" as before.
