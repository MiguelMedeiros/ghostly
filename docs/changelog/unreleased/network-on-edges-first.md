section: Fixed / Groups
---
- After the network is turned off and on again, a group's members come back in seconds again. The groups' links now start ahead of the chats, and the chats wait (4 s at most) until the groups have read their members and their answers have gone out. Before, the chats' first messages took the relays' budget, the groups' answers were held back, and members came back minutes later or not at all. An answer the relays held back is also no longer taken as a sign that direct connections fail on this network.
