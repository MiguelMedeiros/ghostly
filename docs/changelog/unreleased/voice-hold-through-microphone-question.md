- Holding the mic while the browser asks for the microphone (the iPhone's web app asks again at each launch) no longer leaves a recording that can't be sent: once the microphone is allowed it records hands-free, and a tap on Send sends it. Before, it kept recording with nothing to let go of, or was thrown away. A microphone already allowed is still held and sent on release, however slow it is to open (a Bluetooth headset).
