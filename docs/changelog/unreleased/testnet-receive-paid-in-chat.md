- On Testnet, an invoice from the wallet's Receive screen can now be received: a contact who pays it from its card in your chat tells your wallet, which then takes the test sats. Their app keeps that word until your app can take it (yours closed or offline, or their payment still going through), and two invoices paid one right after the other are both received. Before, the contact's test sats left their wallet and your screen kept "Waiting for the payment…" until the invoice was dropped.
