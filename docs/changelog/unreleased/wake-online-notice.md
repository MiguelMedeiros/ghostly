section: For users / Chat
---
- The installed web app, closed, can now tell you a contact came online: "Ana is online" (or "A contact is online" when it doesn't know the name), at most once every 30 minutes per contact, never for muted chats or groups. Settings → Notifications, "Notify me when a contact comes online", right under "Wake me while closed": on by default, and it can be turned off. The contact's app sends the push, so your browser's push service learns when your contacts come online.
