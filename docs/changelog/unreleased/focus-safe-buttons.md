section: Fixed / Everywhere
---
- A button pressed with the keyboard that turns off while it works, or once what it saved is unchanged, keeps the focus, also when you switch to another tab or app while it works: a chat payment sheet's Request and Send, renaming or adding a device, a profile's backup and delete, your status, a connection's code check, and others. The focus went to the page, also when the work failed, so the next Tab started over at the top.
