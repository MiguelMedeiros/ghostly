- Contacts see each other's presence and a set status: a dot while you use Ghostly, and a status you pick from the account menu (busy, away or Do not disturb, an emoji, a short text and when it ends) beside your name in their chat. Settings, "Share when I'm online", hides the dot, and Do not disturb lets messages in without a sound or a notice.
