---
section: For users / Groups
---
- In a group, a member's name shows once over each run of their messages, as in WhatsApp. A new run starts after another member's message, a group notice, a pause of more than five minutes or a new day.
- Members' colours are given out over the group's member list, so in a group of up to 12 no two members share a colour, and every member sees the same colours. A colour can change when someone joins or leaves.
